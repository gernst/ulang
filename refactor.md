# Refactoring of self/lex.u and self/parse.u

Open items, after the literals as unrolled folds (6a3e02c), with Scala dropped and `make test` running every file with `ulang.bin`.

## Tests without the Scala oracle

1. **An oracle for the parser** (TODO, Self-hosted toolchain 2): `parse.u` has no tests of its own, and the parser is only tested by parsing the files of `make test`. Replacement: a printer of programs as source, `show_cmd` and the like, with the test that parsing the printed program gives the same commands, for the library, `tests/`, and `self/`, plus tests of single constructs with the expected trees, as `lex_tokens` has for tokens: tuple types flat and nested, argument lists, chains of operators of each associativity, negative literals, displays with rests, set and map literals as their unrolled folds, `{}`, comprehensions as folds with both forms of generators, and the errors that only the parser reports. The printer could take `show_type` and `show_pat` from `print.u`, together with `qualified` of `syntax.u` as `show_name`, leaving `print.u` with values only.

## parse.u

2. **Names**: `declarations` → `without_tests`, `differing` → `differing_field`, `base_notations` and `Context.base` → `prelude_notations` and `prelude`, and `last_part` → `last_of`.
3. **Layout**: the signatures of `climb_rest`, `parse_set_or_map_elements`, `parse_element`, `comprehension_folds`, `parse_generator`, `parse_map_comprehension`, and `parse_constr` exceed 120 characters, and get the layout of `parse_operators`.
4. **`Field(name, constr, position, arity, typ)`** for the tuples `Id * (Name * Int * Int * Type)` of the accessors and updaters, in `differing`, `type_of_field`, and `fields_named`.

## Groundwork for planned features

5. **Positions per command** (TODO, Self-hosted toolchain 1): the error tests have no positions, and the parser knows the offsets that `check.u` and `compile.u` lack, so this is the most pressing feature.

## Elsewhere

6. **State about modules in three shared structures**, replacing the factoring of `Ctx` of `ctx.u` and `Context` of `parse.u`: the structure of modules is encoded by `CModule`, `CImport`, `CExport`, and `CImplementation` among the commands and decoded again by each phase (`modules_of`, `own_notations`, and `Section` in `parse.u`, `flatten`, `modules`, `own_decls`, `imports_in`, `visibility`, and `sections_of` in `check.u`, `module_names` in `ulang.u`), and the tables of the checked program are built twice (`Prog` of `run.u`, `Globals` of `compile.u`, both with `constr_arity`, and `ordered_by` in check, run, and compile). Per-command state (`sub`, `pending`, `chosen` of `State`, `Gen`, bindings) stays in each phase. In this order, after the uncommitted work on `check.u`, `ctx.u`, `run.u`, and `compile.u` is committed:
   1. **`Decls`**, the tables of the checked program, built once by `decls(program)`: functions by name and arity, procedures, constructors with arity and position, and the orders, equalities, and shows; `Prog` is `Decls` with `computing` and `args`, and `Globals`, the arities of `compile.u`, and the repeated `ordered_by` go.
   2. **`Interface`** of a module, `Interface(notations, ctx, private)`: its own notations, filled by the parser in place of `Context.modules`, and used by `print.u`, its exported declarations, filled by the checker as in item 8, and its private names for messages; every phase keeps a `Map[Id, Interface]`, and a module sees its own declarations and the interfaces of its imports and of the prelude, in place of `visibility`, `State.table`, `hidden`, and `public`.
   3. **`Module`** in `syntax.u`, `Module(name, imports, sections)` with `Unsectioned(cmds)` or `Sectioned(export, implementation)`, and `Source(modules, main)` with the modules in load order, each after its imports, as the output of the parser, so that the four markers leave `Cmd` and their decoding goes; `CNotation` stays among the commands, which it applies to, and the checked program stays a flat `Program(cmds)`. Best together with positions per command (item 5), where `Module` can carry the file. Unsectioned modules remain, as main programs and helpers of tests, which the printer must print unchanged.
7. **The other modules of `self/`** with the conventions of this refactoring, starting with a survey of `check.u`.
8. **Interfaces of modules instead of `hidden`** (TODO, Modules 3): `check_unit` adds all declarations of a module to the shared `Ctx`, private ones too, and every later module starts from `public(before)`, which subtracts the one set `hidden` from all namespaces, so a hidden constructor `C` also hides the abstract type `C`, and the filtering is redone per module. Plan, after the uncommitted work on `check.u` and `ctx.u` is committed:
   1. Survey whether anything but the messages of `unknown_error` needs private declarations of other modules (`members_of` of abstract types, `order_of`, `e.f`, `check_orders`, the synthesized `T.compare` and `T.==`).
   2. `Checked.ctx` is the interface, built from the export section: exported types (datatypes with constructors and members, abstract types without, aliases) and exported values (signatures, constructors, accessors and updaters of exported datatypes), each namespace filtered by its own names; everything without `export` and in the prelude.
   3. Private names only for messages: a map from base names to private declarations, used by `unknown_error`, in place of `hidden` of `Ctx`.
   4. `check_unit` starts from `before`, without `public`; `check_sections` returns the export lists in place of `hidden` and `hidden_sorts`, which `check_orders` uses.
   5. Tests: annotations by `Counter` and `Tally` in `tests/exports.u`, the messages of `tests/errors/private-*.u` unchanged, `make test`, `make testc`, and `make selfcompile`.
   6. Then optionally `Notations` and `Context` of `parse.u` abstract again, CLAUDE.md on `hidden`, and Modules 3 deleted from `TODO.md`.
9. **The prelude with sections**: `prelude.u` imports `option` and `list` so that every module sees them, since each module sees the prelude with its imports (`visibility` of `check.u`), and in the middle, since while the prelude is parsed, its imports are parsed with its notations so far, which `list.u` and `option.u` need. Plan: `prelude`, `option`, and `list` as the implicit unit that every module sees, set by the driver, where `option.u` and `list.u` are parsed after the prelude with its notations, as other library modules are, and the prelude has an export section (exceptions, notations, signatures) and an implementation; since `++` of strings is a builtin whose notation the prelude declares (cc08721), the prelude no longer depends on `list.u`. This removes the parsing with the notations so far, and leaves unsectioned only main programs and helpers of tests. Alternative: the prelude keeps its imports, which are parsed with the notations of its export section.
10. **Consolidations of `parse.u`**, agreed but not done: one `parse_comprehension(in fold, init, add, out expr)` for the comprehensions of sets, maps, and lists; `accessor` and `updater` built together, with a shared match of the fields and `missing_field`, with `Field` of item 4, where `field_type` and `type_of_field` coincide; the helpers `expr_var`, `expr_app`, and `pat_var` of `syntax.u` for the `EVar(Var(unqualified(…), THole))` and the like of `parse.u`, with the access `.f` and the update `.f:=` as `expr_app`, while strings are not formatted in `syntax.u`, so its `qualified` moves to `print.u` as `show_name`. `declare_commands` stays an explicit match rather than a fold.

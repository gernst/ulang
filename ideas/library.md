# Library

1. **Sockets are of TCP only, blocking, and one at a time**: `io_connect` and `io_accept` of `ulang/prelude/io.u` give streams without TLS, which a mail client needs for submission, and without timeouts, and a server of several clients cannot wait for several streams at once, since the language has no threads. Solution: TLS through `openssl s_client` as a process, by builtin procedures of `os` that start one and wait for it, until a binding of a library is worth it; an `io_poll` over a list of streams, which gives those that can be read without blocking; and a timeout of a stream in milliseconds. Networking is an effect that signatures do not show (Later 1).
2. **The costs of the library are invisible** (ubox, Refactor 5B5): nothing says which functions take one pass and which copy or search, so an agent wrote four slow programs that passed every check and test, found only by measuring on large inputs: `distinct` for `sort -u`, quadratic, 20,000 lines killed after 12 s against 108 ms by the runs of the sorted list; `sextets(…) ++ encode64(rest)`, a `++` of strings per step of a recursion, 10 MB of base64 past 60 s; `slice(s, width, size(s))` of the rest per line; and `index_of(str(List(c)), alphabet())` per character, 1 MB of `base64 -d` in 16 s against 0.9 s by arithmetic on `int(c)`. Solution: the cost in the doc of each function that does more than one pass, `++` and `slice` of strings a copy, which `@sig` shows where agents look, as the doc of `distinct` of `ulang/prelude/list.u` now says n log n; and the smells of Refactor 19. `distinct`, `index_of`, `starts_with`, `ends_with`, `trim`, and `sort` were made cheaper since (a65f009, 0a299f0).
3. **Tools of the system lack what the library does not offer** (ubox and the mail client of 16 November 2026): the name of the program, which `os_args` omits, so a tool cannot dispatch by the name of its link as BusyBox does; an empty directory and the metadata of a file, its size, time, and mode, so no `mkdir`, `touch`, `stat`, or `ls -l`; `sleep`; processes, so no `xargs` or `env CMD`; operations on the bits of `Int`, so checksums such as `cksum` and `md5sum` are impractical; and a formatting of dates, so `date` prints UTC by its own arithmetic. Solution: builtin procedures of `os` for the name of the program, a new directory, the metadata of a path, and sleeping, functions of the bits of `Int` in the prelude, dates in a package of `library/`, and processes with item 1.
4. **Bytes and growing text have no builder**: the base64 of ubox holds its bytes as a list of `Int`s, a cell of 48 bytes per byte, and builds its output by `++`, so that compiled it is 63 to 82 times slower than coreutils, the largest gap of its commands, where `sort` is 8 and `wc` 5 times slower. Solution: base64 over `Array[Byte]` by index, a change of ubox; and a builder of strings, a builtin `Buffer` of characters whose result is one allocation, for output built piece by piece.

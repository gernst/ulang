# Diagnostics and refactorings

1. **What does not parse is kept only as tokens**: the text of a part that does not parse, `AUnparsed` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, is its tokens joined as the source usually spaces them (`joined` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), which `format` prints for an example that does not parse. Solution: the text cut from the source by the offsets of the tokens, for the language server of item 9 and the refactorings of item 4.
2. **Hints are text**: the errors give the form to write after `; ` of their message for the habits of other languages that the parser and the checker recognize, but an agent or the VS Code extension cannot carry it out, and the missing cases of a match are given one at a time, by the witness of `NotExhaustive`. Solution: suggested edits as refactorings (item 4), e.g. all the missing cases of a match, or an annotation for an ambiguous use, listed with the diagnostic as edits of item 4 and carried out by `refactor fix`, as the edits of the lints of item 5 are.
3. **The resolution of names is invisible**, and finding the uses of a declaration needs reading, since `size` in a file that imports `set` and `map` may be any of three. Solution: the checker records, for each use that the parser places, the qualified name it resolved to and its type, by the positions that the checked program keeps; then `ulang.bin resolve <file> <L.C>`, the qualified name, type, and declaration of the use there, also as hover in the VS Code extension; `ulang.bin refs` of a use or of `module.name/arity`, all its uses in the files that import it; `ulang.bin sig <module>`, the exported signatures without bodies. The findings are a table of their own, by the spans of item 11, not fields of the tree: for each use its resolution and type, for each local its type, and for each procedure its raises, also the inferred ones of private procedures; `ulang.bin hints <file>` prints them all as JSON lines, in the shapes of `InlayHint` and `Hover` of LSP, from one check, and the VS Code extension keeps them per version of the document and shows inlay hints of the types of locals and of the raises, and hover, from them, refreshed on save, until the server of item 9 serves the same table.
4. **No refactorings**, so renaming a declaration means finding its uses by hand, and the edits that the lints and the hints of errors propose can only be printed. Solution: one package of refactorings, the only way, besides `format`, in which the tool edits sources, whether an agent names an operation or a lint or an error proposes the edit: a datatype of edits of the parsed tree, a part replaced, a statement or command inserted, moved, or deleted, and a name renamed at its declaration and uses, printed by `format_program`, which keeps docs since they are syntax; refused unless the files are formatted, which `check` ensures; checked again after; and refused if any other use resolves differently from before, which makes them safe under overloading without reasoning about it. `ulang.sh refactor <operation> …` and `ulang.sh refactor fix [<path> …]`, the edits that the lints and errors of the files propose, print a diff, or write the files with `--apply`. In order: the frame of edits with `refactor fix` for the lints of item 5, then rename, filling in the missing cases of a match, adding a field to a constructor with its constructions and patterns, and changing a signature with its calls; not extracting or inlining, which edits of the text do well enough.
5. **The lints know only the syntax**: `ulang.sh lint` (`ulang/lint/lint.u`) rewrites the parsed module, so its patterns cannot ask for types or resolved names, `Some` and `value_or` are those of the library only by convention, a list of a list, `List(for x in xs)`, is not found, the same projection `List(for x in xs yield x.name)` repeated and a collecting loop `xs := xs ++ List(v)` instead of a `Buffer` are no rewrites of one expression, and the warnings are only printed, with a replacement only where it is one expression, so `LocalTooHigh`, `UnusedVariable`, and `UnusedPrivate` give none. Solution: lints that look up the resolved name and the type of a part of the parsed module by its position in the checked program, which keeps the positions but not the forms, which it translates to folds, nor the source that `format` prints; each rule gives its finding with the edits of item 4 instead of the one expression of `Warning.replacement`, from the same match, so that the place found and the edit stay together, while the linter never applies them: `refactor fix` of item 4 does, with the re-check and the comparison of resolutions of every refactoring, and the warnings left in the sources for it are its first cases; a lint is kept only if the module checks with its edit; and the compiler may apply the rewrites as the optimizations of Compiler 3. The rules that need the e-graph of the prover are planned in `ideas/lint.md`. Still open among the walks: a `match` of one irrefutable case, which is a `let`; a `case _` over a datatype that grows, `Cmd`, `Expr`, or `Stmt`, which `project.u` would name in a new field of `Project` (Code 1); an `if contains(k, m)` before a `get(k, m)`, one `match` on `get`, which the sources have no instance of yet; appends in loops elsewhere than to an emptied list, which suggest a `Buffer` and need the types, since `++` of buffers looks the same; and the formals of private declarations that are never read, which cannot be `_`, since a function passed as a value keeps its arity. And a warning cannot be suppressed: a line in the doc of a command or `case`, `// lint allows ShowName: …` with a reason, that is itself a warning where it suppresses nothing, for a place such as `show_in` of `ulang/run/run.u`, `utf8`, a standard name whose digit is no index, which `NumberedName` warns on, and `show_fun`, the function of a show hook, which `ShowName` warns on, and the rules that `project.u` turns off below a directory, for the unread locals that tests write on purpose.
6. **A program that imports a package with errors does not run**, even where it never reaches what the errors touch, since `is_runnable` of `ulang/ulang.u` requires its main module to be checked and `check_source` of `ulang/check/check.u` stops at the first package with errors, so that the packages after it are not checked. Solution: check the packages after one with errors against its signature, whose declarations with errors are error nodes, so that each module is checked as far as its imports allow and a run raises `Deferred` where it reaches a rejected definition of an import.
7. **A part that does not parse rejects its whole definition**, so that a run raises `Deferred` at the start of the definition, not where it reaches the part, since checking keeps the commands with such parts as written, with unresolved names (`rejected` and `has_unparsed` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`). Solution: the checker resolves the parts around an `AUnparsed` and keeps it as an error node in the checked body, as it keeps a part that checking rejects, so that only the path through it raises.
8. **Type errors that are follow-ups or name the checker's state**:
    - a function applied to too few arguments, `f(1)` of a two-argument `f`, is `cannot unify (Int, Int) -> Int and Int -> ?2`, where a procedure gets `procedure p expects 1 ins, got 2`;
    - a wrong mode at a call, `call p(in y)` for an out, gives four `CountMismatch`, two of them `expected 0 values, got 1`, instead of one message that `y` is passed as `in` where `p` has an `out`;
    - an unknown constructor in a pattern, `case Just(x)`, also makes `unknown variable x` in the body and `unreachable case None`, and an unknown function also `UnknownFieldOwner` in its lambda;
    - `InfiniteType` prints metas, `infinite type ?5 = ?5 -> ?4`, not the variable of the source, `x`;
    - `OutputUnassigned` stands at the declaration, not at the branch that misses the assignment;
    - a lambda bound by `let` and used at two types gives three mismatches without saying that a `let` does not generalize, which the checker cannot tell from a call of a local of a function type, since a `Local` does not record where it comes from;
    - the hint of an out assigned only inside a `while` is by the names that a loop of the procedure assigns, shared with the lambdas inside it, so a `while` of a lambda that assigns an out of the same name gives the hint wrongly;
    - the hint of `UnknownFieldOwner`, `which an annotation can give`, cannot be followed in a function lambda or a `let`, which have no annotations (Syntax 5).

    Solution: arity of functions as `CountMismatch`; one message per wrong mode; the variables of a pattern whose constructor is unknown bound with `TError`, and the cases after it not judged reachable, as `reaches_error` does for types; the infinite type named by the variable that has it; `OutputUnassigned` at the end of the path that misses it; the origin of a local in `Local`, for the hint of the `let`; the assigned names of a loop per procedure or lambda; and the hint of `UnknownFieldOwner` names what can be written until Syntax 5, a constructor pattern `lambda Point(x, _) => x` or a declared function.
9. **No language server**: the VS Code extension runs `ulang.bin` per action and reads its JSON lines. Solution: `ulang.bin lsp`, a server in ulang over the packages `parse` and `check`, compiled, behind a thin client in `vscode/`: diagnostics and formatting first, then the edits of item 2 as quick fixes, inlay hints of the inferred `raises` and the types of locals, and definition, hover, references, and rename from the resolutions of item 3, completion last. It needs reading standard input in pieces for the framing of JSON-RPC by `Content-Length`, which `os` cannot, columns converted between code points and the UTF-16 units of LSP, and checking cached per package; parsing and checking already continue past errors, as an open file needs. The server wraps the table of item 3 and the placement of comments of item 12, so it comes after them, once `os` reads standard input; it then also checks unsaved buffers and keeps the checked imports, which `hints` cannot.
10. **The field `file` of a diagnostic is the file that was checked, not the file of its module**: `check a.u` of a package whose member `b.u` has an error, of parsing, of checking, or of format, reports `"file": "a.u", "module": "…/b"` at the line of `b.u`, so the file to edit is that of the module, which `parseDiagnostic` of `vscode/extension.js` resolves, while an agent that reads `file` edits the wrong one. Solution: decide whether `file` names the file of the module, for all diagnostics alike.
11. **Positions have no end**: `At` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` holds the `Pos` of the first token of a part, so nothing knows where a part ends, which hover, rename, and the ranges of diagnostics of LSP need, an inlay hint after the name of a local needs, and the placement of comments of item 12 needs. Solution: every `At` carries the span of its part, from its first token to the end of its last, set by the parser and kept by the checker, as the positions are; additive, and done first, so that positions change once, before items 3 and 14, which then may go in parallel.
12. **Comments work only where the tree has a place for them**: a doc is a node, `Doc` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, around commands, constructors, statements, and cases, so the parser rejects every other `//`, at the end of a line, before `end`, `else`, or `catch`, on a `local` line, inside an expression, or above a blank line (`misplaced_comment` and `TMisplaced` of `ulang/parse/lex.u`), and each new place would be a `Doc` in the types that every phase threads. Solution: comments leave the tree, as in gofmt and rustfmt: the lexer keeps every comment with its offset in a list of the module instead of a `TDoc` for the parser to place; `format` puts each back by the spans of item 11, before the first part that starts after it, or at the end of the line of the part that ends before it, where a comment at the end of a line breaks the layout that would keep its part on one line (`case_chain` and the forms of `ulang/format/format.u`); and the doc of a declaration, which hover shows, is the run of comments right above its span, found when asked. So `Doc` goes from `Expr`, `Stmt`, `Cmd`, and `Mod`, the phases after the parser no longer carry it, the round trip of `same_cmds` of `ulang/ulang.u` compares no docs, and `TDoc`, `TMisplaced`, and `misplaced_comment` of `ulang/parse/lex.u` go, as do the rules of `CLAUDE.md` on where `//` may stand; the hard part is the printer, the rest is mechanical, guided by the exhaustiveness errors.

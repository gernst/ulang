# Compiler

1. **Runtime errors have no positions.** Solution: positions in the messages of the runtime and of the interpreter, first for uncaught exceptions and failed `assert`s, from the positions that the checked program keeps (`At` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`).
2. **Programs need a C compiler**, and `cc -O2` takes most of the time of `make selfcompile`. Solution: a backend that writes assembly for x86-64, in progress, steps 0 to 4 done and 5 by hand, see `ideas/assembly.md`, while `make` and `make selfcompile` still go through C and `cc -O2`.
3. **No optimizations.** Solution: passes between checking and the backends, e.g. inlining small functions, folding constants, the rewrites of Diagnostics 5, and loops for folds of comprehensions beyond those over lists and arrays, which the C backend fuses also with several generators (`fusion_of` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`), such as those of sets and maps, and the fusion in the assembly backend, which has none yet (`ideas/assembly.md`, step 6).
4. **A handler may have seen variables from after the body of its `try` in compiled code**: `parse_mod` of `ulang/parse/parse.u` once caught the errors of `parse_command` and of `enter_section` in one `try`, and with clang -O2 the command after one in the wrong section disappeared, correct in the interpreter and at -O0, which was put down to the copies that `compile_try` of `ulang/compile/compile.u` makes before `setjmp`, and worked around by catching `parse_command` alone; nothing was changed in the compiler. But `CLAUDE.md` promises that the handlers see the variables as before the `try`, and the copies, unchanged since `setjmp`, are determinate by the C standard, so the cause was not established. Solution: a reduced case, a test of `ulang/test/statements/` whose handler reads a variable that the body assigns by a call that returned before the raise, run compiled at -O2; then a fix of the translation, e.g. `volatile` for what it restores, or a statement in `CLAUDE.md` of what holds.
5. **Sets and maps take any result of `compare` other than -1 and 1 as equal**, matching `case -1`, `case 1`, and `case _` (`ulang/prelude/set.u`, `ulang/prelude/map.u`), while both backends pass the result of a declared `compare` hook through unchanged (`compare_values` of `ulang/run/run.u`, `compare` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.c`), so with `function compare(a: V, b: V): Int := a.n - b.n`, `contains(V(1), Set(V(5)))` is true. Solution: both backends normalize the result of a hook by its sign, with a case in `ulang/test/datatypes/orders.u`.
6. **`==` differs between the backends inside values of an abstract type that declares `==` but no `compare`**: compiled, a tuple, list, or datatype without its own `==` equates by `compare` of its fields, which raises `Unordered` at such a field, e.g. a `Tally` of `ulang/test/modules/exported.u` in a `List`, while the interpreter equates by `==` of the fields (`equals_ref` of `ulang/compile/compile.u`, `u_equals_by_compare` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.c`, `equal_all` of `ulang/run/run.u`). Solution: a structural equality of constructors in the runtime by `u_equals`, with that case.
7. **Fused comprehensions evaluate in another order than `list.foldr`**: the loops of `ulang/compile/compile.u` take the elements first to last and then the initial value, `foldr` the initial value first and then the elements from the last, which shows only when two parts raise different builtin exceptions. Solution: the order of `foldr`, or a statement in `CLAUDE.md` that the order of evaluation of a comprehension is unspecified.
8. **The binary of the tests exits 1 for failed tests** (`u_run_tests` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.c`), where `ulang.sh test` exits 3, while `CLAUDE.md` says that `make testc` has the outcome of `make test`. Solution: status 3, as `load_status` of `ulang/ulang.u` gives.

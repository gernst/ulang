# Checker

1. **A raise cannot become a value**, so code that collects an error catches each kind into an out, as `load_file` of `ulang/ulang.u` does with the message of each phase, and the long `raises` of the helpers of `ulang/test/cases/cases.u` list every phase. Solution: a statement that gives an `Option` of the exception of a call, or a `try` with a result, and the exceptions of the phases as one datatype of `ulang/syntax/error.u`.
2. **Termination is proven only where the lemmas reach**: the prover proves the termination of the recursive functions that the lemmas of a module reach and unfolds only those (`terminations` of `ulang/prove/termination.u`, `ideas/prove.md`, section 6), `check --prove` reports the call that no measure decreases, and `make termination` lists the functions of `ulang/` that stay open, 82 of them, mostly passed as values, recursing on filtered lists, or on results of functions (`ideas/prove.md`, section 8, step 2); but a measure that the search does not find cannot be given, and the functions that no lemma reaches are unchecked, so the split between functions and procedures guarantees termination only there. Solution: `decreases e` after `:=` as the fallback measure, an obligation of the simplifier at each recursive call; the checker stays out of it.
5. **Unparsed commands are classified by their text** (the review of c079446): `code_free_errors` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` takes an unparsed command for a lemma or test if its text starts with `lemma ` or `test `, which fails for `test\tfoo()`, a line break after `lemma`, or `test(`, so that `compile` refuses a program that it should only warn about. Solution: `AUnparsed` keeps the kind of command that its first token started, which the parser knows.

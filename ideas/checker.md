# Checker

1. **A raise cannot become a value**, so code that collects an error catches each kind into an out, as `load_file` of `ulang/ulang.u` does with the message of each phase, and the long `raises` of the helpers of `ulang/test/cases/cases.u` list every phase. Solution: a statement that gives an `Option` of the exception of a call, or a `try` with a result, and the exceptions of the phases as one datatype of `ulang/syntax/error.u`.
2. **Termination is proven only where the lemmas reach**: the prover proves the termination of the recursive functions that the lemmas of a module reach and unfolds only those (`terminations` of `ulang/prove/termination.u`, `ideas/verification.md`, section 6), `check --prove` reports the call that no measure decreases, and `make termination` lists the functions of `ulang/` that stay open, 82 of them, mostly passed as values, recursing on filtered lists, or on results of functions (`ideas/verification.md`, section 8, step 2); but a measure that the search does not find cannot be given, and the functions that no lemma reaches are unchecked, so the split between functions and procedures guarantees termination only there. Solution: `decreases e` after `:=` as the fallback measure, an obligation of the simplifier at each recursive call; the checker stays out of it.
3. **A local without a type is typed only by the call right after its declaration** (`initialized` of `ulang/check/check.u`): the rule that agents broke most, at five sites in two scripts of the mail client of 16 November 2026 and once among some fifteen declarations of ubox (Refactor 5T6, 5B3), since `var q, r` before one call with both outs checks and `var o, text` before two calls does not, which is hard to keep apart while writing, though `MissingType` says it exactly. Solution: a local without a type takes it from the first statement that assigns it, an out of a call or an assignment, wherever it stands in the block, which definite assignment already orders before every read; until then `@fix` writes the type (Refactor 14).
7. **Two members of a package may declare tests of one name**, which the checker accepts: an `@put` of `test signature_heads()` into `ulang/refactor/test/declarations.u` checked clean while `ulang/refactor/test/handles.u` of the same package had a test of that name, which only the compiled run of the directory showed, as two lines `test signature_heads passed` (the work on the former Refactor 61, 16 November 2026), though CLAUDE.md says that the names of tests are distinct. Solution: the checker reports a test name that two members of a package declare, at the second, as it reports a clash of other declarations, so that `refactor` replies it as a new error.

# Code

1. **Each traversal is written several times**: `ulang/check/check.u` recurses over `Type` by hand in `free`, `has_holes`, `subst`, `occurs`, `zonk_type`, and the `unknown_in_*`, `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` in `sorts_in` and `pattern_names`, and `ulang/prove/syntax.u` walks terms in `rebind`, `subst`, `replace`, `loose`, and `occurs`, each with copies for branches and lists, so that a new field is easily forgotten in one of them, as the guards were in `unknown_in_handlers` and `occurs`. Solution: the traversals stay explicit `match`es, as the rules of style ask, which a reader and an agent change in one place each, and name every constructor instead of their `case _`, which `has_holes`, `subst`, and `occurs` of `ulang/check/check.u`, `sorts_in` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, and `rebind`, `subst`, `replace`, `loose`, and `occurs` of `ulang/prove/syntax.u` still have, as `CLAUDE.md`, Style, asks, so that the exhaustiveness check lists each traversal that a new field or constructor concerns; one function over the children of a term only in the prover, where it keeps the counting of binders in one place.
2. **Facts are kept in several places**:
   - the builtin names in the `builtin` sections, `builtin_names` and `primitive` of `ulang/run/run.u`, `builtin_functions` and `native_procs` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`, the prototypes of `ulang.h`, and the dispatch of `os`;
   - the parameter modes in four places; the notations of tests as text in `ulang/parse/parse.u` and as commands in `ulang/format/format.u`; the mangled names copied into `ulang.h`; and the keys of exception types as descriptors and strings in `ulang.c`.

   Solution: the backends derive their names from `Mod.builtins` and keep only a table from names to implementations, with a test that every declared builtin is implemented; and one definition of each of the others.
3. **Names are compared as strings**: both backends special-case `and`, `or`, and `assert a == b` by their text, and builtins are dispatched by comparing qualified names as strings. Solution: a datatype of the builtins, or a node of the syntax for operators.
4. **Functions sit where the import order once put them**, which packages no longer force: generic code in `ulang/` that belongs to the library, e.g. `normal_path`, `relative_path`, and `has_file` of `ulang/parse/parse.u` in `os`; `starts_at` of `ulang/parse/lex.u` in `str`; and `split_on` of `ulang/ulang.u` in `list`; and functions of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` that one phase uses, `pattern_expr` and `subst_pat` only `ulang/check/check.u`, and `implicit_imports` and `module_dir` only `ulang/parse/parse.u`. Solution: move each to the file of its concern. The library grows only by what is clearly general, `split_on` and perhaps `starts_at`, since every module sees it, so each name there is a candidate of overloading everywhere and part of every compiled program (Build 2); the helpers of paths wait for `os` as a package of its own (Modules 1). Each move and rename updates in the same commit the references of `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, and `ideas/`, by which agents find code, which `checks/docs.u` checks.
5. **`ulang/prove/` was not reviewed for shapes spelled out by hand**, beside its aliases of the builtins in `ulang/prove/syntax.u`. Solution: a review of `ulang/prove/` like that of the rest of `ulang/`, by the rules of pattern aliases in `CLAUDE.md`, Style.
6. **The principles behind items 1 to 4 are not written down**, also: keep information that is rebuilt later, as `Constr` now keeps its fields, keep twin code in step, as `set.u` and `map.u`, and name fuel and limits, as `Limits` of `ulang/prove/simp.u` does. Solution: once the items are done, state them in `DESIGN.md` and as rules of style in `CLAUDE.md`.

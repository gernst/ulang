# Code

1. **Each traversal is written several times**, each with copies for branches and lists, so that a new field is easily forgotten in one of them, as the guards were in `unknown_in_handlers` and `occurs`. Solution: the traversals stay explicit `match`es, as the rules of style ask, and name every constructor instead of their `case _`, by alternatives, `case A | B =>`, so that the exhaustiveness check lists each traversal that a new field or constructor concerns; done, also in the prover, where `ELeaf` of `ulang/prove/syntax.u` names the leaves of `Term`; one function over the children of a term only in the prover, where it keeps the counting of binders in one place. Copies of one walk that remain beside the `case _`: the cases of a match, of a handler, and of a split, checked and compiled by `expr_branches`, `stmt_branches`, `handler_list`, and `proof_cases` of `ulang/check/check.u` and `expr_cases`, `stmt_cases`, and `handler_cases` of `ulang/compile/compile.u`, which want one helper per phase for the scope of a case; the match over `At` that takes `that` of `AOk` and `AErr` and the error of `AUnparsed`, about a dozen times in `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` and `ulang/check/raises.u`, which wants two generic functions; the variables of a pattern, walked by `pattern_variables` of `ulang/check/raises.u`, `pattern_names` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, and `pat_vars` of `ulang/lint/lint.u`; and the names that a command declares, `declared_name` of `ulang/lint/lint.u` beside `cmd_names` of `ulang/lint/lint.u`, the names it reads, and `cmd_names` of `checks/docs.u`, the names it declares, which want one `declared_name` in `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` (the review of c319a1d).
2. **Facts are kept in several places**:
   - the builtin names in the `builtin` sections, `builtin_names` and `primitive` of `ulang/run/run.u`, `builtin_functions` and `native_procs` of `ulang/compile/lower.u` with their uses in `ulang/compile/x86.u`, the prototypes of `ulang.h`, and the dispatch of `os`;
   - the parameter modes in four places; the notations of tests as text in `ulang/parse/parse.u` and as commands in `ulang/format/format.u`; the mangled names copied into `ulang.h`; and the keys of exception types as descriptors and strings in `ulang.c`.

   - the ways to report an error in the checker: `report`, about fifteen direct appends to `errors` in `ulang/check/check.u`, which bypass `reaches_error`, `within`, and `unparsed_use`, and `raise` in the declarations (the review of c319a1d), which want one `report_at(pos, e)`, and `report` wherever checking can go on;
   - the loops that skip tokens to a stop in the parser, `skip_arg` and `skip_stmt`, near copies, and the collecting of `format_token` in `parse_expr_recovering`, `parse_example`, and `skip_command`, which want one `skip_until(stop, out text, ref toks)`, where the text of Diagnostics 1 would be cut.

   Solution: the backends derive their names from `Mod.builtins` and keep only a table from names to implementations, with a test that every declared builtin is implemented; and one definition of each of the others.
3. **Names are compared as strings**: the interpreter and both backends special-case `and`, `or`, and `assert a == b` by their text, and builtins are dispatched by comparing qualified names as strings. And names are encoded in strings, where the names that the phases make carry their parts in the owner of `Owned` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` since Modules 1, the accessors and updaters, the tests of examples and lemmas, the enumerations, and the contracts: `declaring` joins names by `","` into a detail that `declared_by` splits, both of `ulang/parse/parse.u`; and the placeholder module `List("?")` of the pending uses is spelled inline beside `generated_name` (the review of c319a1d). Solution: a datatype of the builtins, or a node of the syntax for operators. And one function each for the encodings left, which needs no design.
4. **Functions sit where the import order once put them**, which packages no longer force: generic code in `ulang/` that belongs to the library, `normal_path`, `relative_path`, and `has_file` of `ulang/parse/parse.u` in `os`, which wait for `os` as a package of its own (Modules 4). Solution: move each to the file of its concern, and update in the same commit the references of `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, and `ideas/`, by which agents find code, which `checks/docs.u` checks. `pattern_expr` and `implicit_imports` stay in `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, which uses them itself, in `implicit_elem` and `seen_modules`.
5. **The principles behind items 1 to 4 are not written down**, also: keep information that is rebuilt later, as `Constr` now keeps its fields, keep twin code in step, as `set.u` and `map.u`, and name fuel and limits, as `Limits` of `ulang/prove/simp.u` does. Solution: once the items are done, state them in `DESIGN.md` and as rules of style in `CLAUDE.md`.

# Syntax

1. **Folds cannot stop early, and the bounded quantifiers are missing**: `and` and `or` short-circuit in all backends, which stays, but a fold always walks its whole container, so `exists` and `forall` of sets, maps, and arrays skip `p` once decided but still visit every element, while those of lists stop by recursion, and a search cannot end at its first hit; and `exists` and `forall` clash with the quantifiers of specifications (`ideas/prove.md`, open point 15). Solution: folds that take a test of the accumulator, after which they stop, `foldl(c, acc, stop, f)`, by which `exists` and `forall` stop at the first decision. Deferred, not convinced yet, since each is one more way to write a loop: bounded quantifiers `forall x in c => e` and `exists x in c if q => e` with the generators of the forms, where the unbounded `forall x: T => e` occurs only in specifications, which read the bounded ones as `forall x => contains(x, c) ==> e`, which translate to the stopping folds. The functions `exists` and `forall` of the containers stay until then.
2. **Errors show pattern aliases expanded**: `NotExhaustive` and `Unreachable` print the constructors that an alias stands for, e.g. `missing Some(false)` or `unreachable case Some(true)` where the case is written `Yes()`. Solution: fold a witness or a case back into the aliases whose stored patterns match it (`PatAlias` of `ulang/check/sig.u`) before printing, preferring the alias that covers most.
3. **The printer never breaks a chain of `++`**, however long, as `description` of `ulang/ulang.u` shows, and the `usage` of a mail client became one line of 250 columns, while a `List(…)` wider than 80 characters gets one element per line, so long texts are written as lists of lines, as the cases of `ulang/test/errors/` are, and a `let` of several long bindings is one line of 300 characters; a second mail client kept six lines longer than 120 characters after `format`, comprehensions of two generators and long `List(…)` arguments of tests, which `check` accepts. Solution: a chain of an infix operator wider than 80 characters has one operand per line, each after the operator, at the indentation of the first operand.
4. **The checker expands lambdas of patterns, which the backends then assume**: `lambda_match` of `ulang/check/check.u` turns the formals of `ELam(formals: List[At[Pat]], body)` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` into variables and a match, so the checked program no longer says what the source wrote, and the backends depend on an invariant that the type does not enforce: `ulang/run/run.u`, `ulang/compile/compile.u`, and `ulang/compile/x86.u` raise `Unexpected` unless `pattern_vars` finds only variables, `translate` of `ulang/prove/translate.u` and `free_vars` of `ulang/compile/lower.u` take `lambda_vars`, which silently gives none for other formals, and the fusion of folds of `ulang/compile/lower.u` matches `PVar` directly. Solution: the checker checks and does not expand; it types the patterns of the formals in place, with their exhaustiveness, and the backends translate a lambda of patterns themselves, by a shared lowering in `ulang/compile/lower.u` for the compilers and by matching in the interpreter and `translate`, so that the checked program keeps the lambda as written; the `_` of procedure lambdas (item 6) follows the same rule. Then review what else the checker expands for the backends, among them the forms and generators (`generator` and `fold_expr`), `for` statements (`items`), `let` and `var` of patterns (`declared` and `block_statement`), `out _` (`kept_outs`), and pattern aliases (`binders`), and decide each by the same rule.
5. **Expressions have no ascription `(e: T)`**, although every pattern may carry its type, `p: T`, and `UnknownFieldOwner` asks for one where the base of an access has no known type yet, as in an argument of a call whose candidates differ. Solution: `(e: T)` in parentheses, which the parser tells from a tuple by the `:`, and which the checker unifies with `T`.
6. **Small gaps of the syntax**: a formal of a procedure lambda cannot be `_`, `lambda in _: List[Cmd] => skip end` is `keyword _ is not a name`, while function lambdas take patterns; keywords that are common English words, `pattern`, `example`, `test`, `data`, `raises`, `requires`, `ensures`, `correct`, `invariant`, and `induction`, are natural names of fields and locals, which tripped agents five times, so the fields `raises`, `requires`, and `ensures` became `raised`, `pre`, and `post`; and lemmas take modes, `lemma l(in x: Int)`, while tests with formals take none, `test t(xs: List[Int])`. Solution: `_` as a formal of a procedure lambda, decided by the design review of 5cd2fe1; the keywords that occur only at fixed places, `correct`, `invariant`, `induction`, `requires`, and `ensures` after `:=`, and `pattern` and `example` at the start of a command or of a line of a spec, contextual, and the rest with a hint of a name to write instead, `raised` for `raises`, `cmd` for `test`; and one form for both heads. A call of an expression and `|` in operators are rejected (`ideas/rejected.md`).

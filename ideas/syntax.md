# Syntax

1. **Folds cannot stop early, and the bounded quantifiers are missing**: `and` and `or` short-circuit in all backends, which stays, but a fold always walks its whole container, so `exists` and `forall` of sets, maps, and arrays skip `p` once decided but still visit every element, while those of lists stop by recursion, and a search cannot end at its first hit; and `exists` and `forall` clash with the quantifiers of specifications (`ideas/prove.md`, open point 15). Solution: folds that take a test of the accumulator, after which they stop, `foldl(c, acc, stop, f)`, by which `exists` and `forall` stop at the first decision. Deferred, not convinced yet, since each is one more way to write a loop: bounded quantifiers `forall x in c => e` and `exists x in c if q => e` with the generators of the forms, where the unbounded `forall x: T => e` occurs only in specifications, which read the bounded ones as `forall x => contains(x, c) ==> e`, which translate to the stopping folds. The functions `exists` and `forall` of the containers stay until then.
2. **Errors show pattern aliases expanded**: `NotExhaustive` and `Unreachable` print the constructors that an alias stands for, e.g. `missing Some(false)` or `unreachable case Some(true)` where the case is written `Yes()`. Solution: fold a witness or a case back into the aliases whose stored patterns match it (`PatAlias` of `ulang/check/sig.u`) before printing, preferring the alias that covers most.
3. **The printer never breaks a chain of `++`**, however long, as `description` of `ulang/ulang.u` shows, while a `List(…)` wider than 80 characters gets one element per line, so long texts are written as lists of lines, as the cases of `ulang/test/errors/` are, and a `let` of several long bindings is one line of 300 characters. Solution: a chain of an infix operator wider than 80 characters has one operand per line, each after the operator, at the indentation of the first operand.
4. **Checked lambdas have patterns as formals whose type does not say that they are variables**: `ELam(formals: List[At[Pat]], body)` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` is shared by the source and the checked program, where `lambda_match` of `ulang/check/check.u` leaves only variables, so the backends, also `ulang/compile/x86.u`, read them by `pattern_vars` or `lambda_vars` at every evaluation of a lambda, and the fusion of folds in `ulang/compile/lower.u` matches `AOk(_, PVar(x))`, an invariant that the type does not enforce. Solution: a type of checked programs of their own, or a checked lambda `ELam` of variables beside a source form of patterns, decided together with the record of resolutions of Diagnostics 3, which also separates what the checker gives from what the parser reads.
5. **Expressions have no ascription `(e: T)`**, although every pattern may carry its type, `p: T`, and `UnknownFieldOwner` asks for one where the base of an access has no known type yet, as in an argument of a call whose candidates differ. Solution: `(e: T)` in parentheses, which the parser tells from a tuple by the `:`, and which the checker unifies with `T`.
6. **Small gaps of the syntax**, each a trade-off: an unused out still needs a variable, since `out _` is rejected; a call takes only a name before its arguments, so `many(p)(out xs, ref s)` is written `let q := many(p)` and `q(out xs, ref s)`; `|` cannot be in an operator, so `<|>` of parser combinators is written otherwise; a formal of a procedure lambda cannot be `_`, `lambda in _: List[Cmd] => skip end` is `keyword _ is not a name`, while function lambdas take patterns; and a keyword is no name of a field or a local either, so the fields `raises`, `requires`, and `ensures` became `raised`, `pre`, and `post`, and a `Catch` is no `catch`. Solution: decide each; `out _`, which drops the value, is cheap.

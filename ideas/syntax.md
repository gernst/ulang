# Syntax

1. **No bounded quantifiers**: `exists(c, p)`, `forall(c, p)`, and `find(c, p)` of the containers stop at the first element that decides, but a quantifier over a container is written as a call with a lambda. Deferred, not convinced yet, since each is one more way to write a loop: bounded quantifiers `forall x in c => e` and `exists x in c if q => e` with the generators of the forms, which specifications read as `forall x => contains(x, c) ==> e`; the unbounded `forall x: T => e` occurs only in specifications, and the parser tells it from the call `forall(c, p)` by the `(`, so the names do not clash. The functions stay until then, and folds that stop early were dropped, since `exists`, `forall`, and `find` stop without them and a search that must stop otherwise is a procedure with a `while`.
2. **The printer never breaks a chain of `++`**, however long, as `description` of `ulang/ulang.u` shows, and the `usage` of a mail client became one line of 250 columns, while a `List(…)` wider than 80 characters gets one element per line, so long texts are written as lists of lines, as the cases of `ulang/test/errors/` are, and a `let` of several long bindings is one line of 300 characters; a second mail client kept six lines longer than 120 characters after `format`, comprehensions of two generators and long `List(…)` arguments of tests, which `check` accepts. Solution: a chain of an infix operator wider than 80 characters has one operand per line, each after the operator, at the indentation of the first operand.
3. **The checker expands lambdas of patterns, which the backends then assume**: `lambda_match` of `ulang/check/check.u` turns the formals of `ELam(formals: List[At[Pat]], body)` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` into variables and a match, so the checked program no longer says what the source wrote, and the backends depend on an invariant that the type does not enforce: `ulang/run/run.u`, `ulang/compile/compile.u`, and `ulang/compile/x86.u` raise `Unexpected` unless `pattern_vars` finds only variables, `translate` of `ulang/prove/translate.u` and `free_vars` of `ulang/compile/lower.u` take `lambda_vars`, which silently gives none for other formals, and the fusion of folds of `ulang/compile/lower.u` matches `PVar` directly. Solution: the checker checks and does not expand; it types the patterns of the formals in place, with their exhaustiveness, and the backends translate a lambda of patterns themselves, by a shared lowering in `ulang/compile/lower.u` for the compilers and by matching in the interpreter and `translate`, so that the checked program keeps the lambda as written; as the `in _` of a procedure lambda already is, which the checked program keeps and the compilers do not bind. Then review what else the checker expands for the backends, among them the forms and generators (`generator` and `fold_expr`), `for` statements (`items`), `let` and `var` of patterns (`declared` and `block_statement`), `out _` (`kept_outs`), and pattern aliases (`binders`), and decide each by the same rule.
4. **Expressions have no ascription `(e: T)`**, although every pattern may carry its type, `p: T`, and `UnknownFieldOwner` asks for one where the base of an access has no known type yet, as in an argument of a call whose candidates differ. Solution: `(e: T)` in parentheses, which the parser tells from a tuple by the `:`, and which the checker unifies with `T`.
5. **Small gaps of the syntax**: lemmas take modes, `lemma l(in x: Int)`, while tests with formals take none, `test t(xs: List[Int])`; and a keyword that starts a command, such as `test`, `data`, `type`, or `pattern`, written as a local, `let test := 1`, gets its hint, `write a name such as check` (`name_for` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), and then a follow-up error, since the parse resumes at the keyword as at a command. Solution: one form for both heads; and after reporting a keyword where a name is expected, the parser reads the keyword as that name and goes on. A call of an expression and `|` in operators are rejected (`ideas/rejected.md`).
6. **A loop leaves early only by a flag**: no `break`, and an exception is no way out of a loop, since a `try` restores every variable that its body changed, so what the loop gathered is lost; so the loop carries a flag in its condition and an `if … else` around the rest of its body, or a `for` guard that tests the flag and walks the remaining elements only to skip them. The sources have six (15 November 2026): `skip_arg` and `skip_stmt` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`, `while not done` with a level of nesting only to avoid the rest of the body; `project_root` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`, which sets `found := true` when the search reaches `/` without finding, the flag misnamed to end the loop; `run_script` of `ulang/refactor/refactor.u`, `for action in actions(script) if failure == None`, and `check_source` of `ulang/check/check.u`, `for mods in … if not raised`, both walking the rest; and `exec` of `ulang/run/run.u`, `while more` around a `match` whose last case sets `more := false`; in five of them code follows the loop, so a `return` would not replace it (`ideas/rejected.md`), and the other `while` loops end by a condition of their own, mostly the next token. Solution: a statement `break`, which ends the innermost `while` or `for`, where the checker joins the variables at each `break` with those at the exit by the condition, as it joins the paths of an `if`, the prover takes the exit of a loop as the invariant and, at each `break`, the facts of its path instead of the negated condition (`invariant_gaps` of `ulang/prove/proof.u`), and the backends jump past the loop, the compiled code popping the handlers of the `try` blocks that it leaves; `break` outside a loop, in a lambda inside one, or in the body of an `assert raises` is a parse or type error, and no `continue`, which the guard of `for` and an `if` give. `break` must first be a keyword, searched for as a name and renamed.

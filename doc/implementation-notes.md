# Implementation notes

The detail of each phase that does not fit the overview of `doc/implementation.md`, by package, each note with the functions that decide it. These notes are to move into the comments of the files that they name; until then, a change of behaviour updates the note of its phase. A construct that crosses phases has a note in each package that handles it, which says what that package does with it.

## ulang/syntax: the tree, the builtins, the notation, and the errors

### Parts and positions

- Every part of an expression, statement, and pattern is an `At` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`: `AOk(pos, span, that)` at the position of its first token or of the token that names it, a call at its name, an operator at the operator, and with its `Span` from its first token to the end of its last, which the parser reads from the tokens, each with the stop of the code before it (`Tokens` of `ulang/parse/lex.u`, `span_from` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), while the translations of the checker take the span of what they translate, and code that a phase makes without one the empty span `span_at(pos)`, and so is each declared formal and local, `At[Var]` at its name, which the phases after the checker read by `vars` and code that the parser did not read places by `unplaced_vars`, while the outs and refs of a call are plain variables.
- `AUnparsed(pos, span, text, error)` is a part that does not parse, which the parser makes, and `AErr(pos, span, that, errors)` a part or a command that checking rejects, with its diagnostics, whose positions may be inside it: a part, when its check ends, takes the errors reported while it was checked that none of its parts took (`take_errors` of `ulang/check/check.u`), so that a mismatch stands on the call, the `if`, or the assignment that finds it, while its diagnostic points at the part, and what manifests after the parts, the pending overloads, the errors of declarations and of raises, stands on the import at its position, else on the command that contains it, else is `Unexpected` (`with_errors`), as the errors of the prover stand on the lemma and on the function whose termination is open (`with_proofs` of `ulang/prove/lemma.u`); patterns and parts at `Pos(0, 0)` take none, and the walk of errors is `cmd_errors` and `part_errors` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, for the source and the checked program alike.
- Positions are never stripped: the checked program keeps them, code that does not care reads `x.that`, and comparisons modulo positions, as the round trip of `format` (`same_cmds` of `ulang/ulang.u`) and the parser tests, take `strip_cmd` and the like, which set them to `Pos(0, 0)` and its empty span, as `nowhere` places code that the parser did not read.

### Paths and names

- A module of a project is known by its path from the root without `.u`, a `Path` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, the list of the names of its directories and its file, which `Name(path, base)` holds as the module of a resolved name, empty for a name without one, and which messages, ` in m`, the field `module` of diagnostics, and hints show with `.`, e.g. `ulang.check.check` (`path_text` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`), while directories and files keep `/` and are converted at the edges (`dir_path` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`); the modules of the library, a directory with `prelude.u`, are members of the package `prelude`, e.g. `prelude.map` (`place_of` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`, `library_module` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`).
- The test of an example is the name `Owned(m, List("f", "example"), "N")` that `owned_text` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` shows as `f.example.N`, as it shows the test `l.lemma` of a lemma, `Owned(m, List("l"), "lemma")`; an accessor is `Owned(m, List("T"), "name")` and an updater `Owned(m, List("T"), "name:=")`, whose owner is the datatype.
- `Mod.imports` are the ids of the imported packages, each at its line (`import_ids` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`), which `seen_modules` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` expands to their members. Every module implicitly imports the whole library (`implicit_imports` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`).

### Shared shapes of constructs

- Comments: the lexer gives every line of a comment as a `Comment` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, with its position, whether it stands on a line of its own and above a blank line, and the next token of code, which the parsed module holds in the order of the source, `Mod.comments`. Each comment is the text of its line without `//` and the space after it, and a blank line follows it if one did. The doc of a declaration, which hover shows, is the run of comments on the lines right above its span, without a blank line between (`doc_of` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`).
- Alternatives `p | q` are `POr` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`. A case with alternatives means the cases of its alternatives in order, multiplied out from the left where they nest, each with the guard and the body (`alternatives` and `expanded_cases` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`), so a guard that fails on one alternative tries the next. Only a case with a guard is multiplied out by the compilers, and only at the alternatives that bind variables, since a guard that fails on the variables of one tries the next (`tested_alternatives` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`). Every reader of the type of a handler takes it apart first, into a handler per alternative of its type (`expanded_handlers` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`), while the compilers split it only at the top, by the types, and each part as the pattern of a case, so that the alternatives inside stay where they stand (`tested_handlers` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`).
- An out of a call may be `_`, no variable of the source: the parser keeps it as the variable of `dropped_out` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, named by the keyword `_`; an in of a procedure lambda may be `_` too, the same variable, which the checked program keeps and the compilers do not bind.
- Procedure types carry their raises as `Raises` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, `RWritten` of the source, which the checker makes `RSet` of the exceptions by name, and `RUnknown` of inference.
- The statements `let p := e, …` and `var p := e, x: T, y, …` are `SLocal(mutable, decls)` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`; `let p := a, q := b in e` is `ELet`; `for` is `SFor(gens, body)` with generators `GVal` and `GPair` and lets `GLet`; forms are `EForm` and `EFor`; a pattern with a type is `PAnnotated` and an ascription `EAnnotated`; a hexadecimal literal is `LHex`, which `hex_text` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u` writes with upper-case digits and without leading zeros, `0xD800`.
- The specs of declarations, their examples and contracts, are the `Spec`, a field of `CFun`, `CFunSig`, `CProc`, and `CProcSig` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`; lemmas are `CLemma` with a `Proof`, a claim `ClaimCorrect`; abstract types stay as commands `CAbstract`.
- The escapes of literals, `\"`, `\'`, `\\`, `\n`, `\t`, and `\r`, are `escapes` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, which the lexer reads and the printers write by `escape`, where a quote is escaped only in a literal that it delimits; the runtime writes them by its own `escape` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.c`, which the test `escapes_round_trip` of `ulang/parse/lex.u` keeps in step in both backends.

### Errors

- Each error is a `Diagnostic` of `ulang/syntax/error.u`, printed as one JSON object on a line by `format_diagnostic`, with the fields `file`, the file to edit, that of the module of the error, from the working directory if it is below it (`diagnostic_file` of `ulang/ulang.u`), `module`, `line`, `column`, `kind`, the name of an `ErrorKind`, `code`, `message`, details such as `expected` and `found`, and `severity` `warning` for a warning, which does not fail the command (`format_warning`), the only output of errors, read by agents and the VS Code extension (`parseDiagnostic` of `vscode/extension.js`).
- Runtime and internal errors have no positions; the texts of the runtime errors that both backends report are `runtime_message` of `RuntimeError` in `ulang/syntax/error.u`, which `ulang/compile/compile.u` writes into every program as the formats `u_text_*` that the runtime prints; `InternalError` of `ulang/syntax/error.u` holds the errors of the interpreter, the checker, and the compiler that checked programs never raise, since an earlier phase rules out what they meet, with `Unexpected(what)` for code without a case of its own; the messages of internal errors are `internal_message` of `ulang/syntax/error.u`. `assert raises` that is not raised fails with `AssertionNotRaised` of `ulang/syntax/error.u`, which cannot be caught.
- A definition that its own errors make unsafe to run, all but those of raises and of proofs (`blocks_run` of `ulang/syntax/error.u`), has an error node of them as its body (`rejected` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`). `compile` accepts only errors that generate no code (`code_free_errors` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`), also an unparsed command whose keyword is `lemma` or `test` (`unparsed_command` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`).
- The exceptions of builtin functions and accessors (`unchecked_exceptions` of `ulang/syntax/builtins.u`) are defects of the program, which no `raises` counts, as functions cannot declare them.

## ulang/parse: the lexer and the parser

### Lexer

- The lexer gives every line of a comment as a `Comment`, apart from the tokens (`lex_all` of `ulang/parse/lex.u`), so the tokens and the parts have none, and the parse and the phases after it do not depend on them.
- It gives a character or token that does not lex as a token `TBad` of `ulang/parse/lex.u` with its error, which the parser reports where it meets it (`expected` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), so that a `||` hides no other error.
- `|` is no operator: the lexer rejects `||` and a `|` next to a character of operators, with their hints (`is_glued_bar` and `character_hint` of `ulang/parse/lex.u`), and the parser hints at a `|` after an expression (`hint_at` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).
- The names of a path after `.` may be keywords, `ulang.test.cases`, which the lexer gives as names (`named_after_dot` of `ulang/parse/lex.u`), so that no keyword of a path starts a command or a statement where the parser resumes after an error.

### The parser desugars nothing

Everything stays as written until the checker, `for` statements, lambdas with patterns, the forms, accesses and updates of fields, `let` of patterns, pattern and type aliases, and ascriptions, so that `format` keeps what the source wrote.

- Datatypes: every argument of a constructor is a named field; a name may repeat in the constructors of a datatype, and must have the same type in each, else a parse error. Fields are read as `EField` and updates as `EUpdate` (from the left); a comma followed by `name :=` continues the updates, also in `assign` and `local`.
- Pattern aliases: `pattern` is a keyword, and an alias without formals is declared and built with `()`, `Zero()`, and matched also bare, `case Zero =>`.
- Forms: `List(…)`, `Set(…)`, `Map(…)`, and `Array(…)`, capitalized as a datatype of one constructor is named as its type, are read by `parse_form` of `ulang/parse/parse.u` into `EForm` and `EFor` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`. Their names are forms only before `(`, in expressions and patterns, and name types elsewhere, `List[Int]`, so that no constructor, function, procedure, or pattern alias takes them, and a name of a form alone, as in `foldr(xs, Nil, List)`, is a parse error (`form_at` and `check_value_name` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`); `list`, `set`, `map`, and `array` are ordinary names. In patterns only `List(x, y; rest)` and `List()` occur, the constructors `Cons` and `Nil`, which are also written so, and which `format` writes as `List(…)`.
- Generators: `for p in c` and `for k => v in m`, guards `if q`, and lets `let p := e` from left to right, one after another without commas, where a let, `GLet` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, is read where a generator may follow (`parse_gens` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`); `yield` is omitted after exactly one generator and no let, which yields what its pattern binds. The parser leaves the field `typ` of `GVal` and `GPair` `THole`, which the checker fills.
- `for` statements are read with one generator and then guards and lets, `for x in xs let y := f(x) if p(y) do … end` (`parse_for` and `is_loop` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).
- Pattern aliases: the alternatives at the top of the pattern of an alias follow `case`, one on each line, as the constructors of a datatype do, `pattern CSig(): Cmd := case CFunSig(…) case CProcSig(…)`, where `|` at the top is a parse error and stays for alternatives inside, `case Some(1 | 2)` (`parse_alias_cases` and `require_cases` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).
- Numbers: `-n` for a number `n` is a negative literal, in expressions and patterns. A number is decimal, or hexadecimal after `0x`, with digits of either case, `0xd800`, also negative, `-0x10`, where `0x` without digits is a parse error; the parser keeps it as `LHex`.
- Ascriptions: `(e: T)` is `EAnnotated`, which the parser tells from a tuple by the `:` after the first expression, and which stands alone in its parentheses, so that a component of a tuple or an argument of a call is ascribed in parentheses of its own, `((a: T), b)`, while `(a: T, b)` and `f(a: T)` are parse errors with a hint (`ascription_hint` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`): one form, where a component could not tell its ascription from the tuple.
- Tuple types: since `(A, B) -> C` is a function of two arguments, a function of one pair is `((A, B)) -> C` (`as_type` and `type_apply` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).
- Raises in types: a comma followed by `name :` ends the raised types of a procedure type, since it starts the next formal (`at_raised_type` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), so a procedure type with raises in a list of types takes parentheses.
- A missing `end` is reported at the construct still open where the parse ends, `the if, or a construct inside it, has no end`, which may be at the end of the input (`expect_end` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).
- Qualified names: the parser reads a qualified name in types, `ulang.syntax.Expr`, and in patterns, `case prelude.option.Some(n) =>`, also bare, which is then never a variable (`parse_qualified_name` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), and in expressions as accesses of fields, `ulang.syntax.size(xs)`, which the checker reads as a qualified name.
- There is no parallel assignment, since a statement `(x, y) := …` would make the parse depend on the layout, the `(` at the start of a line being otherwise the arguments of a name that ends the line before it.

### Specs

- Examples, lines `example …` after the `:=` of the head of a signature in `export` or of a private definition, before the body, after the contracts of a procedure: a signature with a spec is a head, `:=`, and the spec, which the next command follows (`parse_spec` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), and an example goes as far as its expression, whatever the layout, and ends at the next `example`, a command, or what cannot continue it, so that a body after examples cannot start with `(`, `-`, an operator, `.`, or `with`, which a `let` can bind (`parse_example`). An example that does not parse fails its test, not its declaration, so that the file checks and the other examples run, keeping their numbers: the parser keeps it as an `AUnparsed` (`parse_example` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).
- Imports drop the examples of the modules they load (`without_examples` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), and their lemmas, with their tests (`without_tests_of` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).
- Lemmas and their proofs are read by `parse_lemma` and `parse_proof` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`; `requires` and `ensures` of a function are a parse error.

### Recovery

The parser resumes after an error at the next command, at the next statement, and at the next `,` or `)` of an argument, reporting the errors of all of them, which the parsed modules hold as their parts that do not parse, `AUnparsed` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, also the commands (`parse_errors`, `has_errors`, and `require_parsed`, which raises them, of `ulang/parse/parse.u`, `parse_stmt_recovering` and `parse_arg`), each with its tokens as the source lays them out, without the comments (`source_text` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).

- A command whose head does not parse is skipped, while the spec and the body after the `:=` of a function, procedure, or test are parsed for their errors (`skip_unparsed`).
- A keyword where a name is expected is read as that name after its error, by a parse of the command again, so that its uses give one error per keyword and command, `keyword test is not a name, at 2 places`, without the follow-ups of a parse that took it as the start of a construct (`parse_named`).
- Neither the parse nor its errors depend on the layout, only on the tokens, since a statement ends at the next statement keyword, at what ends its body, or at a name after what ends an expression, outside its brackets and blocks (`skip_stmt` and `at_next`), where a missing operand is found at the next token, which may be on the next line.
- A file with parse errors is checked without its commands that do not parse, while a definition whose part after `:=` does not parse keeps its signature (`parse_expr_recovering` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), and a procedure whose contract does not parse keeps its body (`parse_contract_expr`).
- A statement that does not parse may assign every variable, so that the rest of the body is checked without follow-up errors, where a use of a name that one of them declares, as far as the parser read it, is `UnparsedDeclaration` instead of an unknown name and does not stop the definitions (`without_unparsed` and `unparsed_names` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`, `check_partial` and `is_blocking` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- An unparsed command records the keyword that starts it (`started_by` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), by which `compile` takes a lemma or test that does not parse as an error that generates no code.
- The errors give the form to write after `; ` for the habits that they recognize (`hint_at` and `operator_hint` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`, with the cases `habits` of `ulang/test/errors/parse.u`).

### Imports and packages

- `import p.q` loads the package of the directory `p/q` from the root, once, without the tests of its members, where the names of the path have letters, digits, and `_`, also keywords, so no `..` and no `-`, and a string, `import "p/q"`, the form before paths, is a parse error that writes the path; an import of a file `….u`, of an unknown package or one without members, of the own package, of the library, which every module sees without importing it, and a cycle of packages are parse errors (`parse_import` and `import_package` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), where an import that fails stays among the imports of the module at its line, as an error node of its error, which the checker does not see (`import_failures` and `without_unparsed` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`), so that `format` and `refactor` keep it in place.
- The parser gives `Source(mods, packages, main)` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, the modules in load order, each after its imports, as `Mod(name, imports, exported, builtins, implemented, comments)` with the commands of its sections paired with their positions and the comments of the file, and the packages as `Package(id, members)`. Its only state is the source loaded so far, from which each module gets its initial notation, the exported notation of its imports and of the other members of its package (`exported_notation` of `ulang/syntax/notation.u`, `exported_ops` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).
- Loading a module loads its package, all members, each parsed with the notations that the export sections of the others declare, read from their tokens before they are parsed (`load_package` and `package_files` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`); the library is the `.u` files of the directory that `os_library_dir` gives, whose members see each other (`package_files` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`). A module written by `refactor @put` into a directory that does not exist yet is a package of no other members.
- The texts that a script of `refactor` has edited, and the overlays of `--overlay`, are read instead of their files, also in directories that do not exist yet (`parse_file_overlaid`, `overlaid_files`, and `overlaid_text` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`); `parse_texts` of `ulang/parse/parse.u` parses the texts of the modules of one package without files, for the cases of `ulang/test/errors/`.
- The cut load: `parse_file_loaded` of `ulang/parse/parse.u` with `declared` cuts each member of a package other than that of the file to its imports, its export and builtin sections, and the `data` and `type` commands of its implementation section, whose fields give the variances of its abstract types and its private sorts, and a member without sections to nothing (`declared_tokens`).
- The enumeration of the tester is loaded after the library (`load_after_library` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`).

## ulang/format: the printer

- `format_program` of `ulang/format/format.u` prints a module as source; `format_pattern(notation, …)` prints the patterns of the source, which alone keeps the round trip, while `format_pat` prints checked patterns for messages.
- Comments are put back by the spans of the parts (`lined` of `ulang/format/format.u`): a sequence of lines, the commands, constructors, statements, cases, spread elements and arguments, or the lines of a spec, takes the comments of its region, each before the first item that starts after it, at its indentation, or at the end of the line of the item that ends before it, those after the last item before what ends the sequence, and those inside an item that its text does not place above the item; the printer writes each as a marker, by which a sequence finds those that the texts of its items place, and which `format_module` replaces by the comments. A comment that a flat layout cannot place, between the elements of a form, the arguments of a call, the branches of an `if` expression, the operands of a chain of infix operators, the declarations of a `let` or `var`, or the fields of a constructor, spreads the layout, one part per line, and a comment that no layout places, such as one inside a pattern, stands above the line of its part, where it stays. The round trip of `format` compares the texts of the comments in their order (`same_mod` of `ulang/ulang.u`).
- An alternative `|` binds tighter than `: T`, which `format` writes around alternatives in parentheses; a case head wider than 80 characters puts each alternative after the first on a line of its own, under it after `| ` (`case_head` of `ulang/format/format.u`).
- The pattern of an alias with cases is printed by `alias_pattern_text` of `ulang/format/format.u`, and an alias at its uses as written.
- An ascription is printed as it is (`node_out` of `ulang/format/format.u`), qualified paths as the source writes them (`written_type` of `ulang/format/format.u`), specs by `spec_text` of `ulang/format/format.u`, an example that does not parse as its tokens, and hexadecimal literals by `hex_text`.
- A lint replacement is printed by `format_module_expr` of `ulang/format/format.u` at the indentation of its line (`line_indentation` of `ulang/format/format.u`).
- The layout rules of `match`, of forms, of heads, and of `if` are under Layout of `CLAUDE.md`.

## ulang/check: the checker

### The checked program

- The checker resolves every name to its module, also of tests, `CTest(name, …)`, and gives `Prog(cmds, builtins, imports, main)` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, every command of the export and implementation sections of all modules with qualified names, at its declaration, the definitions checked and the rest as written, then the tests of examples and lemmas, the builtin sections apart, the imports of each module at their lines, which hold the errors of imports such as `UnusedImport`, and the module of the loaded file, where the backends and `translate` take `definitions`, without the signatures and the pattern aliases, so that the driver takes the tests and `translate` the lemmas of a module by the modules of their names.
- It checks the members of each package together, once, after their imports, against their signatures (`check_program` and `check_package` of `ulang/check/check.u`, `Sig` of `ulang/check/sig.u`), where the private declarations of the members are in the signature, hidden from the other members by `State.hidden` and `State.hidden_sorts`, and type aliases are declared each after those it names. A use in one member of a private declaration of another is unknown with the hint that it is private to its module (`private_hint` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- The checker resolves names, accesses and updates of fields, and type aliases, and keeps the other constructs as written, with what their expansions need recorded, such as the type of the container of a generator or of a formal that is a pattern, while checks such as exhaustiveness expand them internally; the checked program says what the source wrote, so that the hints, the raises, and the lints read the constructs, not their translations.
- `enter` of `ulang/check/check.u` starts each command with an empty substitution and numbering of unknowns, so a type that a phase records while one command is checked must be zonked before the next; pending entries are solved to a fixpoint at the end of the command, before `zonk` replaces the placeholders.
- `builtin` sections: its uses keep their module, and its types are not abstract types that raise `Unordered` or `Incomparable`, but datatypes without constructors, so `is_data` of `ulang/check/sig.u` holds for `Str`, `Int`, and the others.

### Fields

Fields give accessors `e.name` and updates `e with name := a, other := b`, resolved by the type of `e` to the accessor `T.name` and the updater `T.name:=` (`accessor_name`, `updater_name`, `accessor_field`, and `field_of` of `ulang/check/defs.u`), so that checked programs have neither `EField` nor `EUpdate`, and which raise `MissingField("T.name")` for constructors without the field. The checker declares both for each field of a datatype, without definitions (`declare_fields` of `ulang/check/check.u`), exported where the constructors are, and the interpreter and the compiled code implement them by the position of the field in each constructor (`fields` of `Defs`, `u_select` and `u_update` of the runtime, or `u_field` where all constructors have the field at the same position). The type of `e` must be known when the checker reaches them, else `UnknownFieldOwner`. Patterns bind the variables of the fields' names where they can.

### Forms and generators

- The checker types the forms directly and keeps them as written: a form by the type of its elements and the container of its name, `List[A]`, `Set[A]`, `Map[K, V]` of elements `(K, V)`, or `Array[A]`, reporting a part of another type as `an element of List` or `the rest of List` (`form_types`, `yields`, and `form_rest` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- A generator is typed by the scheme of the fold of the module of the type of its container, whose function gives the type of its items, a pair of key and value for a map, and whose accumulator is the container that the form builds (`generators` and `fold_items`), each generator, guard, and let in the scope of the patterns of those before it, a let checked as the `let` of expressions is, its value against a fresh type of its pattern (`let_step`), with the type of the container of each generator recorded in the field `typ` of its constructor; `k => v` over a container that is no map matches its items as pairs, as `for` statements do.
- A generator infers its container, solves, and takes `foldr` for lists and arrays and `foldl` for the others from the module of its type (`fold_items` of `ulang/check/check.u`), with the formals typed by the type arguments of the container, so `x.f` works in the next generators; a generator over a map with one pattern binds the pair, an unknown type is `UnknownContainer`, and a type whose module declares no such fold of three arguments `NoFold`. Patterns of generators and lets are irrefutable, else `NotExhaustive` at the pattern. The accumulator has a name that users cannot write.
- `for` statements: the checked program keeps the generator, guards, and lets, with the type of the container, each guard and let checked in the scope of the lets before it (`items`, `loop_gens`, and `loop_steps` of `ulang/check/check.u`); its pattern is irrefutable like those of generators, else `NotExhaustive`.

### Patterns

- Pattern aliases: an alias names a pattern whose variables are exactly the formals, each once (`AliasVariables`). The checker types the pattern once in its module, after the constructors and the aliases before it, so aliases do not recur, and stores it with its constructors resolved in `Sig.pat_aliases` (`PatAlias` of `ulang/check/sig.u`, `declare_pat_aliases` of `ulang/check/check.u`); a use, which an alias joins as a candidate next to the constructors, stays in the checked pattern as `PApp` of the alias (`constructor_binders` of `ulang/check/check.u`), whose uses of the modules of the pattern count for `UnusedImport` (`pattern_aliased`); exhaustiveness expands it by its arguments for the formals (`alias_free` of `ulang/check/check.u`), so that errors show the expansion. The `CPatAlias` of each alias in the checked program has its resolved pattern, free of aliases, and follows the function of an alias that builds (`with_alias` of `ulang/check/check.u`). An alias without `_` is also a function of its name, whose body is `pattern_expr` of the pattern (`definition_of`), so `And(x, y)` builds the value; one with `_` only matches (`PatternOnly`), as one with cases does. Aliases share the namespace of constructors and functions; an alias in `export` is exported whole, with its pattern, and must not match private constructors (`PrivateConstructor`), and one in `implementation` is private.
- Alternatives: each binds the same variables with the same types, else `OrVariables` at the alternative. The checked program keeps them, one case with one guard and one body, so that the checker, the raises, and the hints see the case as written: `binders` types each alternative, and the exhaustiveness check splits a row of alternatives into a row per alternative, as Maranget's matrices do, so that `Unreachable` stands at an alternative (`or_rows` and `reachable` of `ulang/check/check.u`). The alternatives at the top of a handler, of `catch` and `assert raises`, match exceptions of their own types, `case Refused(text) | text =>`, each annotated in the checked program by its type, while the handler has the type `THole` (`handler_pattern` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- Annotations: every pattern may have a type, `p: T`, also inside, `(x: Int, y)`, and the formals of function lambdas, `lambda p: Point => p.x`, as patterns, which the checker unifies with the type that the pattern matches before it checks the pattern, so that the annotation resolves the fields and constructors inside, and drops (`pattern_binders` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- Lambdas: the formals of function lambdas are patterns; the checker types those that are no variables as the patterns of one case, checked for exhaustiveness as `let` is, and keeps each, annotated by its type, `PAnnotated` (`lambda_patterns` of `ulang/check/check.u`), where a name of a constructor or pattern alias is no variable, as `binders` resolves it (`is_pvar`).
- The patterns of `let`, of `var`, of generators, and of lambdas are irrefutable, checked for exhaustiveness as the cases of a match where they are not all variables, so tests of refutable patterns, also of pattern aliases such as `And(a, _)`, use `match`.
- A pattern of an unknown constructor or of another arity is `_` whose variables are `TError`, in a match that is not judged for exhaustiveness or reachability (`has_wrong_constructor`).

### Expressions, locals, and statements

- `let p := a, q := b in e` binds patterns one after another, each in the scope of those before it, and a tuple binds in parallel, `let (a, b) := (b, a) in e`; the checker checks the bindings one after another and keeps them in one `let`; no `let` generalizes, since only declarations have type parameters.
- The statements `let` and `var` declare the variables of their patterns from there to the end of the body, one after another as the `let` of expressions, where a `let` has a value for each and binds what nothing assigns after, and a `var` declares variables, also without a value; the checker checks each declaration in the scope of those before it, for the statements after it in its block, where the variables of a `var` are assignable and those of a `let` are not, and a pattern that is no variable is irrefutable, checked for exhaustiveness as the cases of a match (`local_block` and `statement_node` of `ulang/check/check.u`), and keeps them as written, each pattern with the types of its variables and each value checked.
- The assignments of one `a := …, b := …` are sequential, as the declarations of one `let` or `var` are: `b := f(a)` reads the `a` that the statement assigned, which the checker checks one after another (`statement_node` of `ulang/check/check.u`) and each backend and `translate` of the prover assign so.
- An ascription `(e: T)`: the checker checks the expression against the type, which it passes down, so that a part of another type is reported at the part, and the ascription resolves accesses, overloads, and the formals of lambdas, `(p: Point).x`, `size((empty(): Set[Int]))`; the checked program keeps it with the normalized type, as the checker expands nothing for the backends, and the interpreter, the compilers, and `translate` of the prover take it as its expression (`infer_node` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- A hexadecimal literal is translated to `LInt` (`checked_lit` of `ulang/check/check.u`), so that no later phase meets it, and `show` writes every `Int` in decimal.
- An out `_` of a call is kept by the checked call, typed as its out (`out_variables` of `ulang/check/check.u`); a ref, an out of a claim `correct p(…)`, and an out of a call of a lemma in a proof cannot be `_`.
- No formal, local, pattern variable, or formal of a lambda hides a local around it (`HiddenLocal`, `unhidden` of `ulang/check/check.u`), while locals may hide declarations, which depend on the imports; the names of the translations, such as `(acc)` and `(item)`, nest.
- Variables are read only where they are assigned on every path, and outs must be assigned on every path that does not raise, where passing a ref reads it (`Local` of `ulang/check/check.u`, joining the paths of `if`, `match`, and `try`; a `while` may not run its body, and a handler starts from the variables before its `try`). A local without a type gets it from its value, `var x := e`, or as an out of the call right after its declaration, `var q, r` before `divmod(in a, b, out q, r)` (`initialized` of `ulang/check/check.u`), else it is `MissingType` with a hint that says so (`untyped`), since `var a, b` before two calls types only the outs of the first.
- `p(…)` calls a variable `p`, which hides a procedure `p`.
- `assert raises p do … end` fails, with `AssertionNotRaised`, unless its body raises an exception that the pattern `p` matches, typed as the pattern of a `catch` case, and for which its optional guard holds, while another exception is raised further, as from a handler whose guard fails; the variables of `p` are those of the guard only, so a pattern without a guard has none (`RaisesVariables`), and the variables after it are those before, as at the start of a handler, since its body raises if it passes.

### Overloading

- The candidates of a name are its declarations in all visible modules, its own module's no more than the others; locals hide them all, where a call of a local, formal, or out whose type is no function of its arity, while functions of its name and arity are visible, is a `CannotUnify` with the hint that names those the arguments fit, without reading the variable, so that no `Unassigned` follows (`hidden_functions` and `hiding_hint` of `ulang/check/check.u`), and within a module, overloading is only by arity.
- A qualified name `p.x` has as its candidates the visible declarations of `x` of the module of the path `p` and of the members of the package of `p`, which may both exist, as for `ulang.test.modules.paths` of `ulang/test/modules/paths.u`, so it reaches no private declaration of another module, and a path that users cannot write, such as `(test).enumerate`, without visibility (`overloads`, `visible_names`, and `is_qualified_by` of `ulang/check/check.u`); the names that the checker makes, such as the folds of generators, are qualified by the paths of their modules and resolve by the same rule. In expressions the checker reads an access as a qualified name where the first name is no local and the longest prefix names a path that the module sees, the base the name after it and the rest accesses (`qualified` of `ulang/check/check.u`), so a formal, local, or pattern variable named as the first name of such a path is `QualifierLocal`.
- Declared hooks `show`, `compare`, and `==` are no candidates: uses take the builtins, which reach the hooks through the types of their arguments (`is_hook` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- An applied name infers its arguments first, then keeps the candidates of its arity whose instance unifies with them and the expected type, each tried on a copy of the substitution, where a clash with a part of type `TError`, which `report` drops, still rules a candidate out, since `trials` and `fits` of `ulang/check/check.u` count every error that `report` sees, so `size` of a `List[TError]` is `list.size`. One remaining candidate is committed; with several, the use gets a fresh type, a placeholder name, and a pending entry.
- Pending entries are solved to a fixpoint at the end of the command, and lets do not generalize their types. A use resolves uniquely, by its types alone, without preferences: since the substitution only grows, a candidate that does not fit never fits again, so a use with one candidate left is committed, whatever the order of solving, e.g. `map.put` of a map literal, which then fixes the type of its `map.empty` when `set` and `array` are imported too. No candidate is an error, and several at the end of the command are ambiguous, both listing the candidates with their types.
- Lambdas that are arguments, of functions and of procedures, wait until the other arguments and the type of the function or procedure are known, and then take the types of their formals from it, so `exists(xs, lambda q => q.x == 3)`, `(lambda p => p.x)(Point(1, 2))`, and `each(in lambda in s, ref n => … end, xs, ref n)` check (`infers_first`, `infers_lambdas`, and `checks_lambdas_last` of `ulang/check/check.u`); a lambda that is bound by a `let` has no expected type, while the declared type of a local, an assignment, or a result gives one, `let g: Point -> Int := lambda p => p.x`, also to a procedure lambda, whose formals without a type take theirs from it before its body is checked (`typed_proc_formals`), and so does an annotation of a formal, `lambda p: Point => p.x`, or an ascription, `(p: Point).x`, which the hint of `UnknownFieldOwner` suggests (`hint_of` of `ulang/check/message.u`).
- Two visible types of one name make an unqualified use `Ambiguous`, and the use writes the path of the one it means, `ulang.compile.Gen` beside `Gen` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, or `prelude.Int` beside the `Int` of `ulang/test/modules/own_int.u` (`visible_names` of `ulang/check/check.u`).

### Modules and imports

- An import of a package of which the module uses nothing is the type error `UnusedImport` at the import, which does not block a run, judged after the package of the loaded file checks without errors, whose members have their tests, and only if the source parses, since a part that does not parse may use an import: a package is used by the modules of the resolved names of the checked commands of the module (`used_modules` of `ulang/check/uses.u`), of the pattern and type aliases that it uses, which checking expands and records, and of the resolved patterns of the pattern aliases that it declares, also of those with `_`, which have no definition (`aliased`, `alias_uses`, and `unused_imports` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- Definitions repeat the signatures of `export` exactly, else `SignatureMismatch` at the definition names the first part that differs (`Differing` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- The cut load: `check_cached` of `ulang/check/check.u` with `declared` only declares the packages other than that of the file, their exported functions and procedures by their signatures (`signed_decls`), whose commands stay error nodes with the types that the signature gives them, which the hints of the importers read (`declared_cmd`), and neither takes them from its `Checks` nor adds them; the signatures that they show, the diagnostics of the file, and its hints are those of the whole load, while the errors of their definitions are not found, which the tests of `ulang/test/declared/`, which `project.u` runs only compiled, check on a package of each kind of declaration. It serves `check` without `--test` and `--prove`, `hints`, and the checks of `refactor` with the resolutions of its `@resolve`, `@refs`, and `@rename` (`checked_diagnostics` of `ulang/ulang.u`, `file_hints` and `parsed` of `ulang/refactor/refactor.u`).

### Specifications

- Examples: the checked program has no examples. An example that does not parse has a test that raises the error of parsing as a `Str` (`example_body` of `ulang/check/check.u`). The checker translates each into a test `f.example.N`, `N` from 1 per base name in the order of the source, after the definitions of the package, also with errors, but none of a function or procedure whose definition has errors, whose examples would be follow-ups of them, so that a change that breaks a definition and makes an example stale shows both at once (`example_tests`, `failed_owners`, and `example_stmt` of `ulang/check/check.u`), checked as written, as a test of the module, so that its names take the declarations that overloading chooses; by convention an example applies its function, which nothing checks, so `example size(List(1)) == 1` written on `set.size` tests `list.size`. An example that raises a type its call cannot raise is `NeverRaised` at the example, while builtin exceptions, which no `raises` counts, are not checked. Examples on the definition of an exported name are `ExampleOnDefinition`, and an example of `export` is checked as an importer sees the module, without its private declarations (`example_commands`).
- Lemmas: `lemma l[A](in x: A, …) := requires a, … ensures b`, with `requires` optional and the modes of a procedure in the head, where `out` formals are existential, given by an assignment `y := e` in a proof and bound by the outs of a call `l(in a, out q)` to witnesses, locals `var q: T` without a value, and `ref` is rejected. A proof, see `ideas/verification.md`, section 4, has steps `let` and `var`, calls of a lemma whose statement checks or of the lemma itself as an induction hypothesis, assignments, and `assert`, then at most one split, `induction`, `match`, or `if`, whose cases are proofs (`check_proof` of `ulang/check/check.u` and of `ulang/prove/proof.u`). The checker checks each assumption and `b` as a `Bool` over the formals, as the body of a function, so that the checked lemma has resolved names and folds instead of forms, and keeps lemmas in a table of their own, `Sig.lemmas`, apart from the values, whose names they may share, since only proofs cite them (`declare_lemmas` and `command` of `ulang/check/check.u`, `LemmaSig` of `ulang/check/sig.u`). Lemmas are private, in `implementation` sections and files without sections, `export` rejects them, and the interpreter and the compiler skip them.
- Contracts of procedures, `requires a, …` over the ins and refs and `ensures b` over all formals, follow the `:=` before the examples, on the signature in `export` of an exported procedure, which its definition does not repeat (`ContractOnDefinition`, `with_contracts` and `contract` of `ulang/check/check.u`), and the checked program keeps them in `Spec` of the `CProc`.
- Claims: a lemma `lemma l(in x: A, …, out y: B, …) := correct p(in a, …, out y, …)` claims that every run of the call that ends normally from a state that satisfies the `requires` of `p` satisfies its `ensures`, where the outs of the call are those of the lemma in their order (`ClaimOuts`), and `p` is defined before it in its package (`ClaimOrder`); its proof is an `invariant e` per `while` of the body of `p` in the order of the source, each over the variables in scope at its loop (`loop_positions` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, `with_scopes` and `claim_proof` of `ulang/check/check.u`), and none is the invariant `true` of every loop. A claim holds only for the runs that end, so it is no fact of the lemmas after it and no lemma that a proof calls (`ClaimCalled`), else `ensures false` of a loop that does not end would prove them all, and it has no test `l.lemma`.
- The generated tests of lemmas and properties are passed to `check_all` of `ulang/check/check.u`, which checks the generated commands of each package after its definitions, if they check without errors; their qualified names resolve without visibility (`overloads` of `ulang/check/check.u`).

### Raises: ulang/check/raises.u

What a procedure may raise is checked after its package, if it has no other errors, by `raises_errors` of `ulang/check/raises.u` over the checked commands and the tests of examples, which keep their positions, giving the first error of each command, which `check_package` of `ulang/check/check.u` adds to those of the package:

- A raised value is of a datatype without type parameters, also a builtin one such as `Str`, else `NotAnException`, and so are the types of `raises`.
- A statement raises the types of its `raise`, the `raises` of the procedures it calls, also through variables of procedure types, and what a `try` lets through: the types that its unguarded handlers do not cover, by the exhaustiveness check (`covers_all` of `ulang/check/check.u`), where `_` covers all, and what its handlers raise; `assert raises p if g` is a `try` of the one handler `p if g`, which covers nothing with a guard. A handler of a type that its body cannot raise is `NeverRaised`, reported per alternative of a handler (`handler_alternatives` of `ulang/check/raises.u`), and an `UndeclaredRaise` of a type that unguarded handlers around it catch in part names a value that none of them catches, `no handler around it catches EmptyBody of ClientError` (`missed_hinted` of `ulang/check/raises.u`, `missed_value` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- A procedure with `raises`, an exported one, or one without formals, an entry of `run` and `compile`, which raise nothing without them, raises none of the others (`UndeclaredRaise`), and a private one with formals and without `raises` raises what its body does, inferred for all of them together as the least fixpoint (`infer_procs`), which its `CProc` in the checked program carries as its raises (`with_inferred` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- Procedure types carry their raises without polymorphism over sets: a procedure used as a value, whose scheme has `RPending`, which each instance numbers (`proc_scheme` and `instantiate` of `ulang/check/check.u`), and a lambda, `raised` of `EProc`, get an unknown, numbered across the package. Unification compares two sets at once and records the other pairs as `Bound` of `ulang/check/raises.u`, where `expect` gives subtyping, the raises of the type found within those expected, flipped for the arguments of functions and the ins of procedures, and both ways for refs and the params of datatypes that a field takes where values flow in, an argument of a function or an in or ref of a procedure, while the others are covariant (`unify_toward` and `Direction`, `with_variances` of `ulang/check/check.u`, `fixed_of` of `ulang/check/sig.u`), and an unknown of a type that values reach from several places, the cases of a `match`, the branches of an `if`, or the elements of a form, is bound to a copy whose raises are unknowns of their own, so that it raises what each of them raises (`bind_toward`).
- The pass solves each unknown as the least that the procedure values and lambdas of its type and the bounds give it, and reports what a value raises that a set of its bounds omits, `UntypedRaise` at the value or the raise in the lambda, and `WiderRaise` where a value of a fixed type brought it in, and `check_package` writes the solutions into the checked program, an unknown without bounds as none (`zonked` and `solved_type` of `ulang/check/check.u`), so that the hints show them and no unknown reaches a later phase. So a procedure value bound without a type raises what it raises, and one of a type that raises less fits a place that allows more.
- The inferred raises are what `hints` shows after the formals of a private procedure (`Exceptions` of `ulang/check/raises.u`).

### Orders, equality, and show: ulang/check/defs.u

- A function `compare` or `==` of two values `T[A, …]` of a datatype `T` of its own module, with distinct type parameters, is the order or equality of `T` (`order_of` of `ulang/check/defs.u`), which the builtins `compare`, `==`, and `<` use for all values of `T`, also inside other values and in generic code; other datatypes compare structurally.
- Only abstract types declare `==`; datatypes with visible constructors may declare `compare`, which must agree with structural equality, unchecked. With `compare` alone, `==` is `compare(a, b) == 0`.
- Comparing values of an abstract type whose module declares no `compare` raises `Unordered`, and if it declares no `==` either, comparing and equating them raise `Incomparable`, since the structure of an abstract type is no order: the checked program keeps the abstract types as commands `CAbstract`, by which `unordered_of` of `ulang/check/defs.u` names the exception, which the interpreter raises and compiled code by `u_unordered` and `u_incomparable` of the runtime, in the descriptor of the type (`ulang/test/datatypes/orders.u`, `ulang/test/modules/modules.u`).
- A function `show` of one value `T[A, …]` of a datatype `T` of its own module, with distinct type parameters and the result `Str`, is the show of `T`, which the builtin `show` uses for all values of `T`, also inside other values; otherwise `show` gives the text of a value, strings in quotes.
- A function `show` of one value, or `compare` or `==` of two, must be a hook of these forms, else `MisshapenHook`, since uses do not reach it otherwise. The hooks are `Hook` of `ulang/check/defs.u`, with their names, arities, and results, from which `order_of`, the schemes of the builtins of their names, `is_hook`, and the message derive.
- The runtime finds these through the type descriptor of each object (`struct u_type` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.h`, one `t_` per datatype, pointing to the declared functions or the structural ones), the interpreter through the maps of `Defs` of `ulang/check/defs.u` from datatypes to them.
- A missing procedure that `run` or `compile` is to call is the runtime error `UnknownEntry` of `require_entry` of `ulang/check/defs.u`.

### Errors of the checker

- A type error is reported at the innermost part around it, and a part of the wrong type at the part, with the type expected and found, e.g. `1 + true` as `expected Int but found Bool in argument 2 of the call` at the `true`, and `if 1 then 2 else 3` at the `1`, and `check` reports all type errors of the first package that has some, at most 20 per file.
- Type errors are reported at the innermost part around them, since every part of an expression, statement, and pattern has its position (`infer`, `statement`, and `binders` of `ulang/check/check.u`), where the checker records each error by `report` and continues, and a part of the wrong type is a `Mismatch` at the part (`expect`), the innermost one, since `check` passes the expected type down to the components of a tuple, the elements of a form, the body of a `let`, and the arguments of a call of one candidate whose result fits it (`infer_expected`, `apply`, and `checks_args` of `ulang/check/check.u`), else at their declaration, with the module `m` if it is other than the file, of an imported package or a sibling (`ImportError` of `ulang/parse/parse.u`, `Located` of `ulang/check/check.u`). The messages of type errors are `located_message` of `ulang/check/message.u`; the hints of habits are `hint_of` of `ulang/check/message.u` and `Hinted` of `ulang/check/check.u`, with the cases `hints` of `ulang/test/errors/types.u`.
- A part of an expression that does not parse, and an unknown name, have the type `TError` of `ulang/syntax/syntax.u`, which unifies with every type and makes no error of what it reaches (`reaches_error` of `ulang/check/check.u`). No phase stops at an erroneous part: each treats it as it treats a part that does not parse, and the raises are checked over the commands without errors.
- A call that no declaration fits has the type `TError`, so that its uses make no follow-ups: a call of a function or constructor of another arity is one `CountMismatch`, `function g expects 2 arguments, got 1` (`callee_text` of `ulang/check/check.u`), a call of a procedure of other numbers of ins, outs, or refs one `CallMismatch` with the type of the procedure, which checks no argument against a formal and takes an in that is a variable not assigned yet, such as an out of the procedure around, as an out, neither read nor left unassigned (`declared_call` and `unassigned_variable`), `+` of a string one `Mismatch` with the hint of `++` (`is_plus`), `NoOverload` lists its candidates, a lambda passed to an unknown function or to one of another arity has formals of `TError` (`infer_lambda`), an infinite type names the innermost variable whose type would contain itself (`named_infinite`), an out that one branch leaves unassigned, while another assigns it, is reported at that branch, the `if` without an `else`, the branch, the case, or the handler (`gap_of`), and a call of a local bound by `let` to a lambda whose arguments do not fit has the hint that a `let` does not generalize and the result `TError` (`let_hinted`, by the field `bound_lambda` of a local); while the other parts with errors keep the types of their declarations, which their consumers check (`application`, `choose`, and `unify_result` of `ulang/check/check.u`); the errors of a translation, as of a form, stand on the part that it translates (`translated`); and the commands of a package whose check raised are kept as written, as error nodes without errors of their own (`unchecked`).
- Errors are deferred: each package is checked against the signatures of those before it, also of those with errors, and only a package whose check raised stops the packages after it (`check_source` of `ulang/check/check.u`).
- When `check` passes plainly wrong code, the errors that `check_all` of `ulang/check/check.u` returns and `diagnostics` of the checked program differ by an error that the tree lost.

## ulang/expand: the translations for the backends

The interpreter, the compilers, and `translate` of `ulang/prove/translate.u` each take `expanded` of `ulang/expand/expand.u` of the checked program first, and expand a lambda of patterns where they meet it (`expanded_lambda`).

- Forms (`for_expansion`), from the recorded types: a literal is unrolled, `Set(a, b)` is `set.insert(a, set.insert(b, set.empty()))`, `Map(k => v)` `map.put(k, v, map.empty())`, `List(a; r)` is `Cons(a, r)`, and `Array(…)` is `array.from_list` of a list; a generator is the fold of its container, a guard is an `if` and a let an `ELet` around the rest of the fold. The rest `r` after `;` is the container that the elements are added to, where the new entries of a map win, and arrays have no rest.
- `for` statements give the backends `SFor(List(GVal(pat, items, typ)), body)`, where `items` is the list of the elements, the container itself if it is a list and else `List(for (item) in c)` by the folds of generators, and the guards and lets are `if`s and blocks of locals around the body (`loop_expansion` and `guarded_body` of `ulang/expand/expand.u`).
- Pattern aliases are expanded from the `CPatAlias` of each alias (`alias_expansion`), so that `ulang/run/run.u` and `ulang/compile/compile.u` see only constructors.
- Lambdas: by `expanded_lambda` of `ulang/expand/expand.u`, `lambda x, (a, b) => e` is `lambda x, (1) => match (1) case (a, b) => e end`, with formals of names that users cannot write, as the folds of generators match theirs, so that the backends bind only variables.
- `let` of expressions gives one binding per `let` (`let_expansion`), which the backends bind directly; the statements `let` and `var` are given as those blocks (`block_expansion`).
- A call with outs `_` is a block around it with a local of a name that users cannot write for each `_` (`call_expansion`), so that the backends see ordinary locals.

## ulang/run: the interpreter

- The interpreter walks the list of the items of a `for`; it binds the alternatives of a pattern by the first that matches (`match_into` of `ulang/run/run.u`), which gives the first of the cases of the expansion, and tries the cases of the alternatives only if the guard fails, from the first, so the guard of the alternative that matched runs twice (`select` of `ulang/run/run.u`); it splits handlers by `expanded_handlers`.
- It keeps the aliases in `Run` for the tests that it expands.
- It raises the runtime error `Deferred` with the first of its errors where it reaches a part with errors (`require_checked` of `ulang/run/run.u`), so that only the paths through such a part raise, while a part that does not parse stays in the checked body of its definition.
- The builtins are `builtin_names` of `ulang/run/run.u`; `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=` are computed by its primitives, with a test of small integers first and the operands of `compare` in the order of the definitions (`defined_builtins` of `ulang/run/run.u`).
- Values: a byte is `VByte`, an array `VArr` of `Val`, a stream of the host `VIO`, a listener `VListener`, a window `VWindow`, all of `ulang/run/run.u`.
- Functions without parameters are computed once, interpreted and compiled.
- `assert a == b` reports a failure with both sides; `show` writes the forms, `List(1, 2)`, `Set(1, 2)`, `Map(1 => "a")`, `Array(1, 2)`.
- With `--timeout`, a deadline is checked at each call and each round of a `while` (`Deadline` and `require_time` of `ulang/run/run.u`); what the tests of `refactor` print goes to stderr (`print_all` of `ulang/run/run.u`).
- The evaluator may not terminate, so it is written as procedures.

## ulang/compile: the compilers to C and to x86-64, and the runtime

### Both backends

- The names and analyses that both backends share are in `ulang/compile/lower.u`; the walks over expressions and statements are written twice, in `ulang/compile/compile.u` and `ulang/compile/x86.u`, as the cost of the assembly backend, which stays and grows with the language, toward registers, ARM64, and unboxed integers (Compiler 2 and 3 of `ideas/compiler.md`). `make` and `make selfcompile` build from C until the assembly build is as fast, since the interpreter of an assembly build runs about 5 % slower, and C stays the portable backend, also of ARM64 and macOS, and the seed `bootstrap/ulang.c`.
- Comprehensions: `ulang/compile/compile.u` builds list comprehensions over lists, arrays, and strings, also of several generators, by nested loops without intermediate lists, where a let of a variable between the generators, which the fusion records as a generator of the fold `let_fold_name` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`, binds its variable in the loop around it (`fused_body` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`), while a let of another pattern leaves the comprehension to its folds, array literals by `u_mk_array`, and array comprehensions by loops that fill an array sized by the first generator, grown for further generators and shrunk at the end unless it is exact, and an array comprehension over other sources, sets, maps, options, buffers, and the types of programs, inside the `foldr` of each, whose function pushes each element into an array that grows, reversed once at the end, since a strict `foldr` applies its function from the last element to the first, and which assumes that a fold passes its accumulator on once (`built_array` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`, `u_array_start`, `u_array_push`, and `u_array_reversed` of the runtime, by internal names of `builtin_functions` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`), in both backends.
- The order in which a comprehension evaluates its parts is unspecified, since only which of two builtin exceptions it raises can tell it: the loops take the elements first to last and then the rest after `;`, while the `foldr` of its translation, which the interpreter runs, takes the rest first and then the elements from the last, so tests expect no exception of a comprehension where two of its parts raise different ones.
- `for`: the compilers walk an array or a string by index instead of the list of its items (`indexed_items` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`).
- Alternatives: the compilers test the alternatives where they stand, taking at each `|` the first that matches, which is the first of the expansion, since the tests of a pattern are a conjunction over the paths of the value and none depends on a variable, and declare the variables of the case before a test that assigns them, in C as the operands of one expression, which hold since no value is `NULL` (`match_expr` of `ulang/compile/compile.u`), and in assembly into slots of the case (`match_pat` of `ulang/compile/x86.u`); a case with a guard is multiplied out into alternatives tested one after another, each assigning the variables of the case and repeating the guard, while the body is compiled once (`case_stmts` of `ulang/compile/compile.u`, `shared_alternatives` of `ulang/compile/x86.u`); and the last case of a match without a guard, which exhaustiveness makes match what the cases before leave, and the irrefutable pattern of a `let` or `for` test only all but the last alternative at each `|`, as a `case _` tests nothing (`case_alternatives` of `ulang/compile/compile.u`, `case_test` of `ulang/compile/x86.u`), and compile a `let` of a pattern with alternatives as a match of one case.
- Effects: compiled code copies outs and refs in and out, and saves the variables that the body of a `try` assigns before its `setjmp` in the slots of its handler, `saved` of `struct u_handler` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.h`, never in locals of C, whose copy and restore clang -O2 and gcc -O3 drop (`save_vars` of `ulang/compile/compile.u`). Compiled code calls a procedure value through `u_call_proc` and the wrappers `wp_`.
- Builtins: the interpreter and the runtime (`builtin_functions` and `proc_name` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`) define, make, order, compare, and show the builtins and builtin types; `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=` are computed by the runtime, with a test of small integers first (`builtin_functions` and `is_runtime_call` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`); the seed knows only the builtins of its `native_procs` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`.
- Names in C: the modules of the library keep the names of their files, e.g. `t_list_46_List` and the key `"os.OsError"`, as the runtime and the seed refer to them, and the other names keep `/` between the names of their paths, which no base contains, unlike `.` (`c_text` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`). A project with a directory `prelude/` at its root clashes with the library in C.
- The C is `static` except `main` and what the runtime declares extern (`runtime_referenced` of `ulang/compile/lower.u`).

### C

- C is a small typed syntax with a printer that adds parentheses (`ulang/compile/c.u`); string literals are `U_LIT` of `"…"`, `u"…"`, or `U"…"` by their greatest code point (`format_str` of `ulang/compile/c.u`).
- Never add `volatile` to the generated C or to the runtime (Working rules of `CLAUDE.md`, `BUGS.md`).

### x86-64

- `assemble_program` of `ulang/compile/x86.u` assembles the checked program in the instructions of `ulang/compile/asm.u`, as data, not built from strings, and `encode_object` of `ulang/compile/elf.u` encodes an ELF object.
- The assembly is position-independent, calling the runtime and the C library by `@PLT`, and keeps every variable and temporary in a slot of its frame, so that no value stays in a register across a `setjmp`, where a `try` saves the variables that its body assigns in slots of their own, which its handler restores; the layouts of the structures of `ulang/runtime/ulang.h` that it reads are written once in `ulang/compile/x86.u` and checked by the `_Static_assert`s of `ulang/runtime/ulang.c`, so that a change of the header fails when the runtime compiles instead of breaking programs.

### The runtime, ulang/runtime/

- Type descriptors: one `struct u_type` per datatype, `t_`, with `compare`, `equals`, and `show`, mirrored by the maps of `Defs`; fields by `u_select`, `u_update`, and `u_field`; `u_unordered` and `u_incomparable` for abstract types.
- Strings are stored in units of 1, 2, or 4 bytes, the narrowest that holds their code points, so that equal strings have equal units (`u_str_at` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.h`).
- A byte is an object of `u_byte_type`, of which each byte exists once.
- Streams are objects of `u_io_type` with its `FILE`, or of `u_socket_type` for a connection, which it reads through its `FILE` and writes unbuffered by `send`, so that a write to a peer that has closed raises `OsError` instead of `SIGPIPE` (`send_all` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.c`), and the collector closes one that is unreachable; listeners likewise, objects of `u_listener_type`.
- Windows: `ulang/runtime/window.c` opens GTK 4 by `dlopen` at the first `window_open`, so that neither the build nor a program without windows needs GTK; a window is an object of `u_window_type`, whose `FILE` is the state of the window.
- The tests of a compiled suite are run by `u_run_tests` of `ulang/runtime/ulang.c`.
- The runtime sets the free-space divisor of the collector to 1, which collects less often than the default 3 at a larger heap, unless `GC_FREE_SPACE_DIVISOR` of the environment is set.
- gprof does not see time in shared libraries such as libgc (Working rules of `CLAUDE.md`).

## ulang/prove: the prover

- The checked program is translated by `translate` of `ulang/prove/translate.u`, which keeps its resolved names, since the builtins of the prover are pattern aliases over the names that checked programs carry (`ulang/prove/syntax.u`), while the sequents `Seq` and `Concl` are declared in `ulang/prove/simp.u`. `translate` gives a branch per alternative of a pattern, each binding the variables in its own order, and assigns the assignments of one statement sequentially.
- A recursive function that the lemmas reach, also one that reaches itself only through a function value that a reflexive datatype holds, such as `Box(f: (Box, Int) -> Int)` (`reflexive_sorts` of `ulang/prove/translate.u`), unfolds only if the prover proves that it terminates (`terminations` of `ulang/prove/termination.u`), else it stays uninterpreted, with an error at the call that no measure decreases.
- Claims: the translation runs the body of the procedure over the terms of proofs (`claim_of`, `run`, and `run_loop` of `ulang/prove/translate.u`): an assignment is a definition of a new variable of its name, an `if` or `match` a split whose cases carry the rest of the body, a call the call of the contract of its procedure, which may be the procedure itself, its outs and refs values that nothing is known of without one, a `raise` the call of the contract `ensures false`, and a loop an `InvariantTerm`, which `invariant_gaps` of `ulang/prove/proof.u` checks by the invariant on entry, its preservation from new values of the variables that the loop assigns, and the rest of the body from the invariant and the negated condition; `for`, `try`, and `assert raises` in the body are gaps `Unsupported`.
- The prover has terms with de Bruijn indices for binders and levels for the variables of open sequents, with one `rebind` and its invariant written down; an e-graph with congruence closure; canonical sums with Fourier–Motzkin elimination and integer tightening; named limits (`Limits` of `ulang/prove/simp.u`); and end-to-end tests that include the goals expected to stay open.
- The errors of the prover stand on the lemma and on the function whose termination is open (`with_proofs` of `ulang/prove/lemma.u`); `OpenLemma` of `ulang/prove/lemma.u` is the exception of a case of `ulang/test/errors/lemmas.u`, whose message of a gap ends with ` at L.C`.
- `make termination` builds and runs `checks/termination.u`, a report, not a test, of the recursive functions of `ulang/` and the library whose termination the prover does not prove.

## ulang/test: the tester

- Lemmas: each lemma whose formals have enumerable types also gets a test `l.lemma` where `check --test` and `compile --tests` load the file, a step before its proof, which can falsify the lemma but never establish it, so the checker generates no such tests and only `check --prove` decides, which runs in both backends before the proofs (`enumerated_tests` and `lemma_stmt` of `ulang/test/generate.u`): it runs the statement on all values of the formals up to one depth, the largest of at most 4 at which the product of their numbers is at most 1000 (`depth_of` and `limits` of `ulang/test/enumerate/enumerate.u`), the first formal in the outermost loop, and raises `Falsified` with the formals of the first case that satisfies the assumptions but not the conclusion, as `name = value, …`, and `Vacuous` if no case satisfies them.
- Values are enumerated by depth, a constructor one deeper than its deepest field, an `Int` of depth `n` from `-n` to `n`, without duplicates and without randomness, so a counterexample is among the smallest: `Int`, `Bool`, `Char`, `Str`, `List`, `Set`, `Map`, and `Array` by `Enumeration` of `ulang/test/enumerate/enumerate.u`, tuples inline, and datatypes whose constructors the module sees and that are not abstract by a function `(enum)m.T` generated into the module of the lemma (`enum_fun` of `ulang/test/generate.u`); type parameters stand for `Int`; a lemma with a formal of another type, a function, a procedure, a `Buffer`, or an abstract type, gets no test.
- The generated code is source with qualified names, which the checker checks after the definitions of the package, if they check without errors. The enumeration is a package of this project, whose examples run as its tests, and the tester loads it into the program under test, after the library, as the module `(test).enumerate` of `enumerate_module` of `ulang/test/generate.u`, which no path names and no module sees, read by `os_library` from the directory next to the library (`load_tester` of `ulang/test/runner.u`), so its names are no candidates of overloading, and the generated tests name them by their qualified names.
- Properties, tests with formals, run as the test of a lemma, where each `assert` outside the body of a `try` or an `assert raises`, whose handlers could catch it, raises `Falsified` with the values of the formals and, of an equation, both sides, and a formal of a type that is not enumerable fails it with `Unenumerable` (`property_stmt` and `with_counterexamples` of `ulang/test/generate.u`); the runners and the backends skip the test with formals, which runs as the test of its name that the tester generates.
- The runner prints the outcomes (`test_module` and `test_outcomes` of `ulang/test/runner.u`), a test that fails by reaching an error by its position (`failure_text` of `ulang/test/runner.u`), and a failed example by its call (`example_failure` of `ulang/test/runner.u`).
- The cases of errors run through the phases by `check_text`, `run_text`, or `prove_text` of `ulang/test/cases/cases.u`, where `check_text` and `check_texts` raise the first `TypeError` of the checker only if each of its errors is one of `diagnostics` of the checked program, what `check` prints, by position and code, else an `Unexpected` of an error that the tree lost (`require_kept` of `ulang/test/cases/cases.u`), not the first, since the diagnostics come in the order of the tree, where a part that finds an error after its parts, a call, a match, or a command, stands before them, while the checker lists them as it finds them.

## The driver ulang/ulang.u

- Commands and their options are read by `request` (`usage`, `paths_request`, `refactor_request`, and `inline_script` of `ulang/ulang.u`); the status of a run is the worst of its files (`load_status` of `ulang/ulang.u`).
- `run` and `check --test` run a program with errors if its main module is checked (`is_runnable` of `ulang/ulang.u`), also if a package that it imports has errors; the file fails with the status of its errors, or 3 if a test fails, `check --prove` proves no lemma if code that the lemmas may reach has errors (`blocks_proofs` of `ulang/ulang.u`), and `compile` refuses it unless they generate no code (`compile_target` of `ulang/ulang.u`).
- The round trip of `format` compares the commands modulo positions and the texts of the comments (`same_cmds` and `same_mod` of `ulang/ulang.u`).

## The library, ulang/prelude/

- The prelude declares the operators, `abs`, `gcd`, `min` and `max` of `Int`s, `first` and `second` of pairs, and the exceptions of builtins: `DivisionByZero` of `/` and `%`; `OutOfBounds(Int, Int)`, index and size, of `at` and the strict `slice` of strings and arrays; `NoCharacter(Int)` of `chr`; `NoByte(Int)` of `byte`; `MissingField(Str)` with the name `T.f`; and `Unordered(Str)` and `Incomparable(Str)` with the name of a type. Its builtin section declares `Bool`, `Int`, `Str`, `Char`, the arithmetic `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`, and `++` of strings; other builtin sections are those of `str.u`, `os.u`, and `array.u`.
- `==`, `compare`, `show`, `and`, and `or` are builtins without a module. `compare` is a total order of all values, consistent with `==`, with results `-1`, `0`, `1`, where constructors are ordered by their position in their declaration; `<` of the prelude is `compare(x, y) == -1`, and `<=`, `>`, `>=` each one `<`, which both backends compute by the runtime and the interpreter by its primitives, so that the prelude, which the prover reads, says what they mean.
- `str`: `chars(s)`, `str(cs)`, `int` and `chr` between characters and code points, `encode_utf8` and `decode_utf8`, `is_digit`, `is_letter`, `is_space`, `size`, `at(i, s)`, `get(i, s)` as an option, `contains(c, s)` of a character and `contains(part, s)` of a substring, the latter of `ulang/prelude/prelude.u`, since a module overloads a name only by arity, `slice`, `starts_with`, `ends_with`, `join`, `split`, `quote`, `number`, `trim` by `is_space`, `lines`, which splits at newlines without the empty line that `split` gives after a last newline, `lower` and `upper` of ASCII, `index_of(part, s)` of the first occurrence, and `replace(s, old, new)` of every occurrence.
  - A `Char` is a Unicode code point, 0 to 0x10FFFF without the surrogates 0xD800 to 0xDFFF, else `chr` raises `NoCharacter`, and a `Str` a sequence of them, which `size`, `at`, and `slice` count; `is_letter` and `is_digit` are ASCII, as names are. UTF-8 is only the encoding of the system: the procedures of `os` decode files, arguments, and names of files, where invalid UTF-8 raises `OsError`, and encode what they write; `encode_utf8` gives the bytes of a string, and `decode_utf8` the string of bytes as an option.
- A `Byte` of the builtin section of the prelude is one of 0 to 255, made by `byte(n)`, which raises `NoByte` outside them, read by `int(b)`, and shown as `byte(n)`. Files of bytes are `Array[Byte]`, read and written by `os_read_bytes` and `os_write_bytes`.
- `map` and `set`: `Map[K, V]` and `Set[A]`, abstract types of AVL trees ordered by `compare`, with `compare` and `==` by their elements and entries; `intersect(s, t)` of sets.
- `option`: `Option[A]` with `or_else`, `value_or`, `map_option`, `exists`, `forall`, `find`, which keeps the value that satisfies its predicate, and the folds of a container of at most one element, so generators and `for x in o do … end` take options, `List(for x in get(k, m) for y in f(x) yield y)`.
- `buffer`: `Buffer[A]`, the elements of a loop that adds at the end, kept as the reversed list: `empty()`, `b ++ xs` for a list `xs`, `size`, and the folds in the order of adding, so `List(for x in b)` reads it out; lexer, parser, and loops of the checker and the compiler use it where a list grows with the program, and `++` of lists where it is bounded by a construct.
- `array`: immutable `Array[A]`: `from_list(xs)`, `size`, `at(i, a)`, `get(i, a)`, `slice`, `contains`, and the folds.
- Lists, sets, maps, and arrays share names and orders of arguments, of which strings have only `size`, `contains`, and the folds, `foldr` and `foldl` of the builtin section of `ulang/prelude/prelude.u`, the module of `Str`, so that generators and `for` go over a string itself, `for c in s`, not over `chars(s)`, which the lint `CharsLoop` of `ulang/lint/lint.u` rewrites:
  - `size`, `contains`, `get` (of association lists for `list`), `remove` (elements of lists and sets, keys of maps), `empty` of sets and maps, `exists(c, p)`, `forall(c, p)`, and `find(c, p)`, which stop at the first element that decides, and the folds `foldr(c, acc, f)` and `foldl(c, acc, f)`, in the order of lists and ascending for sets and maps, where `f` and `p` of maps take key and value.
  - `at(i, c)` of lists, arrays, and strings, with `OutOfBounds(i, size)` outside, where that of lists is `list.at` by `at` of arrays for the error, since functions do not raise; and of lists `take_while`, `drop_while`, and `span` of a predicate, `get_at(i, xs)` of an index as an option, which `get` of arrays and strings is, `sole(xs)` of the one element, and `common_prefix(xs, ys)`.
  - Conversions are forms, `List(for x in c)` of the elements in order, `List(for k => v in m)` of the entries, `Set(for x in xs)`, `Map(for (k, v) in ps)`, where the last entry of a key counts, `Array(for x in xs)`, and `str(cs)` of characters.
  - `infix right 8 ++` and `infix left 7 --`: `++` concatenates lists and strings, unites sets, and joins maps, where the entries of the second count; `--` removes the elements of a list or set, and the keys of a set from a map.
- `os` declares `OsError` and the builtin procedures `os_args`, `os_read_utf8`, `os_write_utf8`, `os_read_bytes`, `os_write_bytes`, where the writes create the missing directories of the path, `os_library`, `os_print`, `os_print_error`, `os_exit`, `os_list` (sorted entries with whether each is a directory, without following links), `os_is_dir`, `os_remove` (a file, a link, or an empty directory), `os_cwd`, `os_chdir`, `os_env` (a variable of the environment as an option), `os_time` (seconds since the epoch), and `os_clock` (nanoseconds of a monotonic clock), of which those that touch files or decode names, all but `os_print`, `os_print_error`, `os_exit`, `os_is_dir`, `os_time`, and `os_clock`, declare `raises OsError`, with `os_walk` and `os_remove_all` of `ulang/prelude/os.u` for the files below a directory, the latter removing them and the directory without following links, so that tests remove what they write below `out/`, and `os_println`, which prints a string by `os_print` and then a newline, while `os_print` prints it as it is, and relative paths start from the working directory.
- `io` declares the builtin type `IO` of streams of bytes, files opened by `io_open(in path, mode, out io)` with a mode of `IoMode`, `IoRead`, `IoWrite`, or `IoAppend`, the streams of the system by `io_stdin`, `io_stdout`, and `io_stderr`, and connections of TCP by `io_connect(in host, port, out io)` and by `io_accept(in listener, out io)` of a `Listener`, the builtin type of `io_listen(in host, port, out listener)`, with `io_port` for the port that the system chose for 0 and `io_close_listener`, read by `io_read_bytes` (at most n), `io_read_until` (through a delimiter), and `io_read_all`, written by `io_write_bytes`, and closed by `io_close`, after which every use raises `OsError`; `io_read_line`, `io_read_utf8`, and `io_write_utf8` of `ulang/prelude/io.u` decode and encode UTF-8 over them in ulang. A stream is passed `in`, since its state is the system's, which a `try` does not undo; streams are ordered by their openings and shown as `<io path>`. The tests are `ulang/prelude/test/os/system.u`, whose sockets connect to a listener of the same program on port 0.
- `window` declares the builtin type `Window` of windows of GTK 4, which raise `OsError` without GTK or a display: `window_apply` patches the widgets of a window by lines of the protocol of `gui/protocol.u`, and `window_next` waits for the next event, a line of the same protocol that a callback of GTK queued, so that no ulang runs inside the toolkit; `ULANG_WINDOW_SCRIPT` of the environment replaces the person by a file of commands, which `window_next` runs when its queue is empty, with shots as PNG, as `window_shot` writes them. The layer of Elm over it is the package `gui/`, a try-out (`ideas/gui.md`).
